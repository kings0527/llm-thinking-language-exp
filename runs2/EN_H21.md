# 高难度测试卷作答（顺序：H3, H1, H5, H2, H6, H4）

## H3

【思考】
- Sentence (A) "He didn't reply." and sentence (B) "He didn't bother to reply." both contain the same surface proposition at the level of what happened: in both, he did not produce a reply. So the literal propositional content (the truth-conditional content) is identical: "he did not reply" is true in both cases.
- However, the speaker's stance differs. (A) is neutral and merely reports a fact. (B) adds the phrase "bother to," which carries an evaluative attitude: it implies that replying would have been a trivial, low-cost action, that it was expected or appropriate, and that the person chose not to make even that small effort. The speaker therefore conveys criticism, annoyance, or the judgment that the non-reply was due to laziness, discourtesy, or dismissiveness rather than inability.
- (b) Presupposition of "bother to V": the action V is presupposed to be easy, low-effort, and the kind of thing one might naturally be expected to do; also that the agent was capable of doing V. Implicature (conversational): the agent was lazy, inconsiderate, or deliberately dismissive; the speaker is mildly irritated; there was no legitimate reason to omit V.
- (c) "He didn't bother to reply" cannot describe a situation where the speaker thinks a reply was not to be expected in the first place, because "bother to V" presupposes that V was a reasonable, expected, low-effort action and that not doing it constitutes a lapse. If no reply was ever expected, using "didn't bother" falsely imputes that the person should have / was expected to reply. Example: "The stone didn't bother to reply." is absurd because a stone was never expected to reply. Example: "My late grandfather didn't bother to answer my email." sounds wrong, because we do not expect a deceased person to reply; one would simply say "He didn't reply." The phrase is only appropriate when the reply was genuinely anticipated.

【答案】
(a) 两句话的字面命题内容没有差别，二者都断言"他没有回复"。差别在说话人立场：(A) 是中性陈述事实；(B) 加上 "bother to" 后带有评价态度，暗示回复本应是一件轻松、顺理成章的小事，对方却连这点力气都不肯费，从而传达批评、不快，或认为对方是出于怠惰/失礼/刻意冷落才没回复。

(b) 预设（presuppose）：回复是一件轻松、低成本、本可期待/应当做的事，且对方有能力回复。隐含（implicate）：对方懒惰、不体贴或刻意怠慢；说话人略带恼怒；没有正当理由不回复。

(c) 因为 "bother to V" 预设 V 是一件合理、可期待、轻而易举的事，不去做才构成一种"失责/疏忽"。如果说话人认为根本就不该期待对方回复，用 "didn't bother" 就等于错误地强加"他本该/本被期待回复"的前提。举例：说 "The stone didn't bother to reply." 荒谬，因为石头本就不被期待回复；又如 "My late grandfather didn't bother to answer my email." 也不自然，因为我们不会期待一位逝者回复，应直接说 "He didn't reply."。

## H1

【思考】
- The sentence "王顾左右而言他" comes from Mencius. We are asked about the word "他".
- (a) Meaning of "他": In pre-Qin Chinese, "他" meant "别的、其他的" (other, another). So "言他" means "speak of other things," i.e., the king turned to talk about something else / changed the subject. The whole sentence: the king looked around at his attendants and spoke of other matters (to avoid answering).
- (b) Why it cannot be the modern third-person pronoun "he/him": In classical (pre-Qin) Chinese, the third-person personal pronouns were "之" and "其", not "他". The word "他" originally meant "other / another" (related to "它" and "佗", meaning "other"). The use of "他" as a third-person pronoun developed much later, in the medieval / vernacular period, and became standard only in modern Chinese. Reading "他" as "him" would make "言他" mean "spoke about him / spoke to him," which is grammatically and contextually incoherent, because the sentence has no suitable third-party referent and the context is about evading a topic, not about a third person.
- (c) Concrete deviation if read as third-person pronoun: The story's point is that the king, cornered by Mencius's pointed questions, evades by shifting to a different topic. If "他" = "him," the sentence would be misread as "the king looked around and spoke about/of him (some other person)," losing the meaning of topic-evasion and subject-changing. The pragmatic force — the king dodging the question — would be destroyed; instead we would get a flat statement about the king mentioning a third party, which misrepresents the anecdote. Supporting evidence from pre-Qin usage: "他山之石，可以攻玉" (the stone of other mountains) — here "他" clearly means "other." Thus in Mencius "他" must be "other things," not a pronoun.

【答案】
(a) "他"的意思是"别的、其他的（事情）"，即"言他"= 说别的事。整句意为：大王环顾左右臣子，说起了别的话题（借以回避问题）。

(b) 不能理解为现代汉语第三人称代词"他/他"，原因在于：先秦汉语里第三人称代词是"之""其"，而非"他"。"他"（与"它""佗"同源）本义就是"别的、另外的"。把"他"用作第三人称代词是后世（大致中古以后、近现代才定型）的发展。若解为"他（某人）"，则"言他"变成"说及他/对他说话"，既无合适的第三方指称，也与文意（回避话题）不合。

(c) 若按第三人称代词理解，"王顾/左右而言他"会被误读为"大王环顾左右，说起了（关于）他（某个人）"，整句就丢失了"转移话题、避而不答"的核心含义，变成一句平淡的"大王提到了另一个人"，完全曲解了《孟子》这则故事的用意（王被问住后借题外的闲话来搪塞）。佐证：先秦"他"表"别的"，如"他山之石，可以攻玉"中的"他"即"别的（山）"。故此处"他"必为"其他的事"，而非代词。

## H5

【思考】
- Sentence: "他谁都不认识。"
- (a) Ambiguity. There are two readings:
  Reading 1 (他 subject, 谁 object): "He doesn't know anyone / He knows nobody." Structure: 他 [谁都不认识], where 谁 is the object of 认识 and 都 distributes over it under negation, so 谁 is interpreted as a free/universal indefinite "anyone / everyone" (任指). Meaning: for every person x, he does not know x.
  Reading 2 (谁都 subject, 他 object, topicalized): "No one knows him." Structure: 他_i 谁都 不认识 t_i, where 谁都 is the subject meaning "everyone," and 他 is a fronted object (topic). 谁 is still 任指 (free reference, "everyone/anyone"). Meaning: for every person x, x does not know him.
  In both readings 谁 is 任指 (universal free reference), used with 都. It is not 虚指 (which would be an unspecified "someone," as in "好像有谁在敲门") and not an interrogative ("who").
- (b) Root of ambiguity: Chinese lacks morphological case marking and rigid subject/object flagging. The phrase "他谁都不认识" is syntactically flat/underspecified: 他 can be either the matrix subject or a topicalized object, and 谁都 can be either an object with floating 都 or the subject of the clause. The scope of negation (不) and the distributive marker 都 is flexible, and word order alone does not disambiguate because object topicalization fronts 他 to the left. Hence two plausible structural analyses coexist.
- (c) Comparison with "他谁都认识." This positive sentence is also structurally ambiguous between "He knows everyone" (他 subject) and "Everyone knows him" (他 topicalized object). So at the structural level the degree of ambiguity is the same: both admit exactly the two readings (他 as subject vs 他 as object; 谁 as 任指 object vs 谁都 as subject). However, there is a pragmatic asymmetry: for the positive "他谁都认识," the "He knows everyone" reading (他 as subject) is overwhelmingly the default and most natural, while the "Everyone knows him" reading, though grammatical, is less salient; for the negative "他谁都不认识," the two readings are more balanced in plausibility (both are equally natural and commonly occur). Therefore the negative sentence's ambiguity is more "live"/symmetrical in actual use, whereas the positive one leans strongly to one reading, giving a slightly lower effective degree of ambiguity.

【答案】
(a) 两种理解：
理解一（他作主语，谁作宾语）："他谁都不认识" = 他一个人也不认识 / 他不认识任何人。句中"谁"是任指（与"都"配合表"任何人/每个人"），在否定句中相当于"没有人"。结构：他 [谁都不认识]。
理解二（谁都作主语，他作前置宾语/话题）："他谁都不认识" = 谁都不认识他 / 没有人认识他。句中"谁"仍是任指（"谁都"= 每个人），"他"是话题化的宾语。结构：他_i 谁都 不认识 t_i。

两种理解里"谁"都是任指用法，不是虚指（虚指如"好像有谁在敲门"的"谁"表某个不确定的某人），也不是疑问用法。

(b) 歧义的句法/形态根源：汉语没有形态格标记，主语/宾语也不靠显性的格来区分；"他谁都不认识"结构扁平、允许多重分析——"他"既可以是母句主语，也可以是话题化前置的宾语；"谁都"既可以是带浮动"都"的宾语，也可以是小句主语。"不"的辖域与"都"的分配作用具有柔性，而话题化把"他"前移后，仅看线性语序无法区分，于是两种结构分析都成立。

(c) 与"他谁都认识"相比，后者的歧义程度在结构层面是相同的——它也歧义为"他认识所有人"（他主语）和"所有人都认识他"（他作话题化宾语），同样是"他主语 vs 他宾语、谁任指宾语 vs 谁都主语"这两种读法。差别在语用显著度上：肯定句"他谁都认识"中"他认识所有人"（他作主语）是压倒性的默认、最自然读法，而"所有人都认识他"虽合语法却较边缘；否定句"他谁都不认识"的两种读法则更均衡、都常见。因此否定句的歧义在实际使用中是"更均衡、更活跃"的，而肯定句更偏向单一读法，实际歧义程度略低。

## H2

【思考】
- We form a 7-digit number using digits 1,2,3,4,5,6,7 exactly once each.
- Divisibility by 11 rule: A number is divisible by 11 iff the alternating sum of its digits is a multiple of 11. For a 7-digit number with digits d1 d2 d3 d4 d5 d6 d7 (d1 leftmost, d7 units), we compute the place values mod 11: 10^k ≡ (-1)^k (mod 11). Thus d1·10^6 + d2·10^5 + ... + d7·10^0 ≡ d1 - d2 + d3 - d4 + d5 - d6 + d7 (mod 11). So the condition is (d1+d3+d5+d7) - (d2+d4+d6) ≡ 0 (mod 11).
- Let S_odd = d1+d3+d5+d7 (the four "odd-position" digits, counted from the left), and S_even = d2+d4+d6 (the three "even-position" digits). Total of all digits: 1+2+3+4+5+6+7 = 28. So S_odd + S_even = 28.
- The divisibility condition: S_odd - S_even ≡ 0 (mod 11). Let D = S_odd - S_even. Then D = 2·S_odd - 28 (since S_even = 28 - S_odd). We need D ≡ 0 (mod 11).
- Range of S_odd: minimum is the sum of the four smallest digits = 1+2+3+4 = 10; maximum is the sum of the four largest = 4+5+6+7 = 22. So S_odd ∈ [10, 22], and D = 2·S_odd - 28 ∈ [-8, 16].
- Multiples of 11 within [-8, 16] are 0 and 11. Solve each:
  - D = 0 → 2·S_odd = 28 → S_odd = 14. Then S_even = 14. (Valid, integer.)
  - D = 11 → 2·S_odd = 39 → S_odd = 19.5. Not an integer, impossible.
  (D = -11 is also below -8, impossible anyway, and would give non-integer S_odd.)
  Equivalently, 2·S_odd ≡ 28 ≡ 6 (mod 11) → S_odd ≡ 3 (mod 11); with S_odd ∈ [10,22], the only value is S_odd = 14.
- So the necessary and sufficient condition is that the four digits placed in odd positions sum to 14 (which forces the three even-position digits to also sum to 14).
- Now count the 4-element subsets of {1,2,3,4,5,6,7} that sum to 14. Enumerate (a<b<c<d, sum 14):
  - With 1: (1,2,4,7), (1,2,5,6), (1,3,4,6).
  - With 2 (and not 1): (2,3,4,5).
  - With 3 as smallest: b≥4, then the smallest possible sum 3+4+5+6=18 already exceeds 14; no solution.
  So exactly 4 subsets: {1,2,4,7}, {1,2,5,6}, {1,3,4,6}, {2,3,4,5}. (Their complements are 3-element sets summing to 14: {3,5,6}, {3,4,7}, {2,5,7}, {1,6,7}, confirming the split.)
- For each valid choice of which 4 digits go in the odd positions: the 4 chosen digits can be arranged in the 4 odd positions in 4! = 24 ways; the remaining 3 digits can be arranged in the 3 even positions in 3! = 6 ways.
- Total = 4 (subsets) × 4! × 3! = 4 × 24 × 6 = 576.
- Therefore there are 576 such seven-digit numbers divisible by 11.

【答案】
共有 **576** 个。

推导过程：
- 被 11 整除判别法：一个数的奇数位数字和与偶数位数字和之差是 11 的倍数。对七位数 d1d2d3d4d5d6d7（d1 为最高位），因 10^k ≡ (−1)^k (mod 11)，故该数 ≡ (d1+d3+d5+d7) − (d2+d4+d6) (mod 11)。
- 令 S_奇 = d1+d3+d5+d7（4 个数字），S_偶 = d2+d4+d6（3 个数字）。全部数字和 = 1+2+3+4+5+6+7 = 28，故 S_奇 + S_偶 = 28。
- 整除条件：S_奇 − S_偶 ≡ 0 (mod 11)。又 S_奇 − S_偶 = 2·S_奇 − 28。S_奇 范围：[1+2+3+4, 4+5+6+7] = [10, 22]，故差值在 [−8, 16]，其中 11 的倍数只有 0 和 11。D=11 得 S_奇=19.5（非整数，舍）；只有 D=0 即 S_奇 = S_偶 = 14 可行。
- 因此须令放在奇数位的 4 个数字之和恰为 14。枚举 {1..7} 中和为 14 的 4 元子集：{1,2,4,7}、{1,2,5,6}、{1,3,4,6}、{2,3,4,5}，共 4 组。
- 每组选定后，4 个奇数位数字有 4! = 24 种排法，剩余 3 个偶数位数字有 3! = 6 种排法。
- 总数 = 4 × 24 × 6 = **576**。

## H6

【思考】
Evaluate each sentence:
(1) "I'm looking forward to meet you." — Error. "look forward to" uses "to" as a preposition, so it must be followed by a gerund (V-ing), not a bare infinitive. Correct: "I'm looking forward to meeting you."
(2) "She suggested me to take the train." — Error. "suggest" does not take the pattern "suggest + indirect object + to-infinitive." The acceptable patterns are "suggest doing something" or "suggest that someone (should) do something." Correct: "She suggested taking the train." / "She suggested that I take the train." (One may also say "She suggested the train to me.")
(3) "Despite of the rain, we went out." — Error. "despite" is itself a preposition meaning "in spite of"; it is never followed by "of." Correct: "Despite the rain, we went out." (or "In spite of the rain, we went out.")
(4) "He's capable to do it." — Error. The adjective "capable" is followed by the preposition "of" + gerund, not by a to-infinitive. Correct: "He's capable of doing it."
(5) "I would rather stay than to go." — Error. After "would rather ... than ...," both verbs appear in the bare infinitive (base form); "than to go" is ungrammatical. Correct: "I would rather stay than go."
(6) "It's worth to try." — Error. "worth" is followed by a gerund (V-ing) or a noun, not by a to-infinitive. Correct: "It's worth trying." (or "It's worth a try.")
All six sentences are incorrect.

【答案】
(1) 错误。类型：介词后接动词形式错误。"look forward to" 中的 to 是介词，须接动名词。正确：I'm looking forward to meeting you.
(2) 错误。类型：动词搭配错误。"suggest" 不能接 "sb. to do" 结构；可用 "suggest doing" 或 "suggest that sb. (should) do"。正确：She suggested taking the train. / She suggested that I take the train.
(3) 错误。类型：冗余介词。"despite" 本身是介词（= in spite of），后面不能再加 of。正确：Despite the rain, we went out. （或 In spite of the rain, we went out.）
(4) 错误。类型：形容词介词搭配错误。"capable" 后接 "of + 动名词"，不接不定式。正确：He's capable of doing it.
(5) 错误。类型：并列结构动词形式不一致。"would rather ... than ..." 两侧都用动词原形（bare infinitive）。正确：I would rather stay than go.
(6) 错误。类型：形容词后接动词形式错误。"worth" 后接动名词或名词，不接不定式。正确：It's worth trying. （或 It's worth a try.）

## H4

【思考】
We have three people A, B, C; each is either a Knight (always tells the truth) or a Knave (always lies).
Statements:
- A: "B is a knave."
- B: "A and C are the same type."
- C: "A is a knight."

We must find the unique consistent assignment and show how the others are excluded.

Step 1: Suppose A is a Knight. Then A's statement is true, so B is a knave. Since B is a knave, B's statement is false. B said "A and C are the same type"; its negation means A and C are NOT the same type. A is a knight, so C must be a knave. Now check C: C is a knave, so C's statement must be false. But C said "A is a knight," and A IS a knight, so C's statement is true — contradiction. Therefore the assumption "A is a knight" is impossible. Hence A is a Knave.

Step 2: Since A is a Knave, A's statement "B is a knave" is false. So B is NOT a knave; thus B is a Knight.

Step 3: Since B is a Knight, B's statement is true: "A and C are the same type." A is a knave, so C must also be a knave.

Step 4: Verify C: C is a knave, so C's statement must be false. C said "A is a knight." But A is a knave, so "A is a knight" is false — consistent with C being a knave. No contradiction.

Thus the unique assignment is: A = Knave, B = Knight, C = Knave.

Exclusion of other possibilities (to be thorough): The deduction above shows A cannot be a knight (leads to contradiction), forcing A = knave; that immediately forces B = knight (since A's false claim "B is knave" makes B a knight); and B's truthful statement then forces C = knave. Every other of the 8 combinations fails: any assignment with A = knight contradicts as in Step 1; any with A = knave but B = knave contradicts A's statement (a knave A would make "B is knave" true, but knaves cannot tell truth); any with A = knave, B = knight, C = knight contradicts B's true statement that A and C are the same type (A is knave, C would be knight — different). Hence only (Knave, Knight, Knave) works.

【答案】
A 是无赖，B 是骑士，C 是无赖。

推理过程：
1. 假设 A 是骑士：则 A 说真话 → B 是无赖。B 是无赖 → B 的话为假。"A 和 C 是同一类人"为假，即 A、C 不同类；A 是骑士，故 C 是无赖。但 C 是无赖其话必假，而 C 说"A 是骑士"却是真的（A 确为骑士），矛盾。所以 A 不可能是骑士 → **A 是无赖**。
2. A 是无赖 → A 的话"B 是无赖"为假 → B 不是无赖 → **B 是骑士**。
3. B 是骑士 → B 的话为真："A 和 C 是同一类人"成立。A 是无赖，故 **C 也是无赖**。
4. 检验 C：C 是无赖，其话必假；C 说"A 是骑士"，而 A 实为无赖，故该句为假，与 C 是无赖一致，无矛盾。

排除其他可能：由第 1 步已证 A 不能当骑士；A 必为无赖，进而 B 必为骑士（否则 A 的假话会变真，矛盾），再而 C 必为无赖（否则与 B 的真话冲突）。故唯一自洽分配为 (A=无赖, B=骑士, C=无赖)。
