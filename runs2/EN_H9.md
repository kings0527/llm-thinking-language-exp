# 高难度测试卷 — 作答（顺序：H3, H1, H5, H2, H6, H4）

## H3

【思考】
(a) Literal propositional content: Both (A) "He didn't reply" and (B) "He didn't bother to reply" assert the same proposition — that the act of replying did not occur. The truth-conditional content is identical. The difference lies in the speaker's stance and attitude. Sentence (A) is neutral: it merely reports an absence of a reply. Sentence (B), by inserting "bother to", expresses the speaker's evaluative attitude: it conveys that replying would have required only a trivial, minimal amount of effort, and the subject chose not to expend even that small effort, implying dismissiveness, laziness, neglect, or a lack of concern/courtesy toward the addressee.

(b) Presupposition of "bother to do X": The construction presupposes that (i) doing X is a small, low-cost, trivial effort; (ii) doing X was in some sense expected, appropriate, or at least reasonable; and (iii) the subject was capable of doing X. Implicature (conversational): By saying he "didn't bother", the speaker implicates criticism of the subject — that the subject was rude, inconsiderate, or deliberately indifferent, and that the failure to reply reflects the subject's attitude rather than inability. It also implicates that a reply was warranted in the situation.

(c) "He didn't bother to reply" cannot describe an action the speaker thinks should not naturally be expected, because "bother to" carries the presupposition that the action is a minor, expected, easily-performed thing. If the speaker believes the action is not at all expected or is in fact extraordinarily difficult, the presupposition is violated and the sentence becomes odd or nonsensical. Example 1: "The baby didn't bother to file his tax return." Filing a tax return is not an action expected of a baby, so we would simply say "The baby didn't file his tax return," not use "bother to." Example 2: "He didn't bother to solve the Riemann hypothesis." Solving that problem is not a trivial, expected task, so "bother to" is inappropriate; one would just say "He didn't solve the Riemann hypothesis."

【答案】
(a) 两句话的字面命题内容相同，都断言"他没有回复"。差别在说话人立场：(A) 中性叙述；(B) 加了 bother to 后，表达说话人认为回复只是举手之劳，而对方连这点小事都不愿做，带有轻慢、怠惰、不放在心上的批评意味。

(b) 预设（presuppose）：回复是件微不足道、本应/合理期待发生的小事，且对方有能力做到。隐含（implicate）：说话人在批评对方无礼、漠不关心、故意冷淡，并暗示"本该回复"。

(c) 因为 bother to 预设该动作是琐细且理应被期待的。若说话人认为该动作本就不该期待（如"The baby didn't bother to file his tax return." 婴儿本无须报税；或"He didn't bother to solve the Riemann hypothesis." 解黎曼猜想绝非小事），预设被破坏，句子就不自然。因此这类情况应直接说 "The baby didn't file his tax return." 而不用 bother to。

## H1

【思考】
(a) In the sentence "王顾左右而言他" (from Mencius), the word "他" means "别的、其他的" (other things / a different topic). The whole sentence means: the king looked to the left and right and spoke of other matters — i.e., he changed the subject to avoid answering.

(b) We cannot interpret "他" as the modern third-person pronoun "he/him" because in pre-Qin Chinese (先秦汉语) the word "他" had not yet acquired the function of a third-person personal pronoun. In the classical period, third-person reference was expressed by words such as 其、之、厥, not by 他. The third-person pronoun usage of 他 developed only much later (roughly from the Wei-Jin / Six Dynasties period onward). Originally 他 (related to 它/佗) meant "别的、其他的" (other / another). Therefore reading it as a personal pronoun is an anachronism imported from modern Mandarin.

(c) If we mistakenly treat "他" as the third-person pronoun, the sentence would become "the king looked left and right and spoke to/about him," wrongly turning "他" into a referential pronoun pointing at some person. But this makes no coherent sense in context: the Mencius dialogue has Mencius progressively cornering the king with embarrassing hypotheticals about a friend, an official, and then the state itself; the king, unable to answer, deflects by switching to another topic. The point of the line is the king's evasiveness — "言他" = "speak of other (matters)." Reading 他 as a pronoun loses the meaning of topic-evasion entirely and introduces a phantom referent that the passage does not support. Hence the correct pre-Qin reading is "别的（事）".

【答案】
(a) "他"的意思是"别的、其他的（事情）"，全句意为：大王东张西望，把话题转到别的事情上（顾左右而言他，即避而不答、岔开话题）。

(b) 不能理解为现代汉语第三人称代词"他"，因为在先秦汉语中"他"尚未发展出第三人称代词的功能；当时第三人称用"其、之、厥"等。"他"（与"它/佗"同源）本义是"别的、其他的"，其第三人称代词用法是后世（约魏晋六朝以后）才产生的，属于以今律古。

(c) 若按第三人称代词理解，整句会变成"大王环顾左右并对他说/说他"，凭空制造出一个指代对象，且完全丢失了"岔开话题、避而不答"的文意。结合《孟子》语境：孟子层层递进追问，王无以应对而转移话题，"言他"正是"说别的事"。理解为代词既无着落、又违背原意。

## H5

【思考】
(a) The sentence "他谁都不认识" is structurally ambiguous and admits at least two readings:

Reading 1 (他 as subject): "他 [谁都不认识]" — He doesn't know/recognize anyone. Here 他 is the subject (the one who fails to know others), and "谁都" is an indefinite phrase in object position meaning "anyone/anybody" (任指). The negation 不 scopes over 认识. Meaning: He knows nobody.

Reading 2 (他 as topic/object): "[他] 谁都 [不认识]" — Nobody knows him. Here 他 is a fronted topic that is logically the object, 谁 is the subject (任指, "anyone"), and the clause means "As for him, anyone doesn't know (him)" = nobody knows him. Meaning: Nobody recognizes him.

In both readings, "谁" is a 任指 (free indefinite) use, meaning "any(one)", not an interrogative and not a vacuous/虚指 use.

(b) The syntactic/morphological root of the ambiguity: Chinese lacks case marking and subject–verb agreement morphology, so the noun "他" can be parsed either as the syntactic subject of 认识 or as a fronted topic that is semantically the object (patient) of 认识. Additionally, the floating quantifier/indefinite phrase "谁都" combined with sentence negation "不" allows two scopal arrangements, producing the two readings. Essentially it is a subject/object (or topic–comment) ambiguity with no morphological disambiguation.

(c) "他谁都认识" has the same degree of ambiguity. It also yields two readings: Reading 1 "他 [谁都认识]" = He knows everyone; Reading 2 "[他] 谁都 [认识]" = Everyone knows him. The structural ambiguity (他 as subject vs. as topic/object) is fully symmetric between the affirmative and negative versions, and the verb 认识 does not block either reading. Therefore the ambiguity degree is the same; the only difference is the polarity of the predicate (affirmative vs. negative), not the number or nature of the readings.

【答案】
(a) 两种理解：
- 理解一（"他"作主语）：他[谁都不认识] = 他谁也不认识（他不认识任何人）。"谁"为任指，相当于"任何人"。
- 理解二（"他"作话题/宾语）：[他]谁都[不认识] = 谁都不认识他（没有人认识他）。"谁"仍为任指。
两种理解中"谁"都是任指用法（"任何……"），既非疑问也非虚指。

(b) 句法/形态根源：汉语缺乏格标记和主宾一致形态，"他"既可分析为"认识"的主语，也可分析为前置话题（语义上是宾语/受事）；加上"谁都"这一任指短语与否定词"不"的辖域可有两种安排，从而产生歧义。本质是主语/宾语（话题—评论）歧义且无形态区分。

(c) 与"他谁都认识"相比，后者歧义程度相同。它也有两读：他[谁都认识]=他认识所有人；[他]谁都[认识]=所有人都认识他。主宾（话题）结构歧义在肯定式与否定式之间完全对称，"认识"一词不阻断任一读解，故歧义数量与性质一致，差别仅在于谓语的肯定/否定极性。

## H2

【思考】
We must count 7-digit numbers formed by using each of the digits 1,2,3,4,5,6,7 exactly once (all distinct) that are divisible by 11.

Step 1: Divisibility rule for 11. A number with digits d1 d2 d3 d4 d5 d6 d7 (from left to right) is divisible by 11 iff the alternating sum (d1 + d3 + d5 + d7) − (d2 + d4 + d6) is a multiple of 11 (i.e., ≡ 0 mod 11). Let S_odd = d1+d3+d5+d7 (the four "odd-position" digits) and S_even = d2+d4+d6 (the three "even-position" digits).

Step 2: Total sum of all digits is 1+2+3+4+5+6+7 = 28. So S_odd + S_even = 28.

Step 3: The divisibility condition is S_odd − S_even ≡ 0 (mod 11). Substitute S_even = 28 − S_odd: S_odd − (28 − S_odd) = 2·S_odd − 28 ≡ 0 (mod 11). Thus 2·S_odd ≡ 28 (mod 11). Since 28 ≡ 6 (mod 11), we have 2·S_odd ≡ 6 (mod 11). Multiplying both sides by the inverse of 2 modulo 11 (which is 6, because 2·6=12≡1), we get S_odd ≡ 6·6 = 36 ≡ 3 (mod 11). So S_odd ≡ 3 (mod 11).

Step 4: Determine possible values of S_odd. S_odd is the sum of 4 distinct digits chosen from {1,2,3,4,5,6,7}. Minimum = 1+2+3+4 = 10; maximum = 4+5+6+7 = 22. So 10 ≤ S_odd ≤ 22. The only value in [10,22] that is ≡ 3 (mod 11) is 14 (since 3 is too small and 3+22=25 is too large). Therefore S_odd = 14, and then S_even = 28 − 14 = 14 as well. (Indeed 2·14 − 28 = 0, exactly divisible.)

Step 5: Count the 4-element subsets of {1,2,3,4,5,6,7} whose sum is 14. Enumerating all C(7,4)=35 subsets and checking the sum, the subsets summing to 14 are:
- {1,2,4,7} = 14
- {1,2,5,6} = 14
- {1,3,4,6} = 14
- {2,3,4,5} = 14
That is 4 subsets. (Each leaves a complementary 3-element subset that also sums to 14, e.g. {3,5,6}, {3,4,7}, {2,5,7}, {1,6,7} respectively.)

Step 6: For each such subset, the 4 chosen digits occupy the 4 odd positions (d1,d3,d5,d7) and can be arranged in 4! = 24 ways; the remaining 3 digits occupy the 3 even positions (d2,d4,d6) and can be arranged in 3! = 6 ways. So each subset yields 4!·3! = 24·6 = 144 valid numbers. Since the digits are 1–7 (no zero), every arrangement is a legitimate 7-digit number with no leading-zero problem.

Step 7: Total count = 4 subsets × 144 = 576.

【答案】能被 11 整除的七位数共有 **576** 个。

推导：被 11 整除的判别法为奇数位数字和与偶数位数字和之差是 11 的倍数。设奇数位（第1,3,5,7位）数字和为 S_odd，偶数位（第2,4,6位）和为 S_even，则 S_odd+S_even=1+2+…+7=28，且 S_odd−S_even ≡0 (mod 11)。代入得 2·S_odd−28 ≡0 (mod 11) → S_odd ≡3 (mod 11)。S_odd 为 4 个不同数字之和，范围 10~22，唯一满足的是 14，故 S_odd=S_even=14。从 {1,…,7} 中取 4 个数和为 14 的子集有 {1,2,4,7}、{1,2,5,6}、{1,3,4,6}、{2,3,4,5} 共 4 组。每组在奇数位有 4! 种排法、偶数位有 3! 种排法，共 4!·3!=144 个，总计 4×144=576。

## H6

【思考】
We evaluate each sentence for naturalness/correctness, identify error types, and give the corrected form.

(1) "I'm looking forward to meet you." — Incorrect. Error type: preposition followed by wrong form. After "look forward to", the word "to" is a preposition, so it must be followed by a gerund (verb-ing), not a bare infinitive. Correct: "I'm looking forward to meeting you."

(2) "She suggested me to take the train." — Incorrect. Error type: wrong pattern for the verb "suggest". "Suggest" cannot take a personal object + infinitive (suggest sb to do). Correct forms: "She suggested (that) I take the train." or "She suggested taking the train." (If you want the "sb + to infinitive" pattern, use "advise" or "recommend": "She advised me to take the train.")

(3) "Despite of the rain, we went out." — Incorrect. Error type: spurious preposition. "Despite" is itself a preposition and is NOT followed by "of". Correct: "Despite the rain, we went out." (The phrase with "of" is "in spite of": "In spite of the rain, we went out.")

(4) "He's capable to do it." — Incorrect. Error type: wrong complement. "Capable" is followed by "of + gerund", not by a to-infinitive. Correct: "He's capable of doing it."

(5) "I would rather stay than to go." — Incorrect. Error type: extra "to" after "rather ... than". After "would rather" and "than" in this correlative construction, both verbs are bare infinitives (no "to"). Correct: "I would rather stay than go."

(6) "It's worth to try." — Incorrect. Error type: wrong complement after "worth". "Worth" is followed by a gerund (or a noun), never by a to-infinitive. Correct: "It's worth trying." or "It's worth a try."

【答案】
(1) 错误。"look forward to" 中 to 是介词，后接动名词。正确：I'm looking forward to meeting you.
(2) 错误。"suggest" 不能接"宾语+不定式"（suggest sb to do）。正确：She suggested (that) I take the train. / She suggested taking the train.（若要用 sb to do 结构，应换 advise/recommend：She advised me to take the train.）
(3) 错误。despite 本身是介词，不能加 of。正确：Despite the rain, we went out.（或用 In spite of the rain。）
(4) 错误。capable 后接 of + 动名词。正确：He's capable of doing it.
(5) 错误。"would rather ... than ..." 后接动词原形，不该有 to。正确：I would rather stay than go.
(6) 错误。worth 后接动名词或名词，不接不定式。正确：It's worth trying. / It's worth a try.

## H4

【思考】
There are three people A, B, C. Each is either a Knight (always tells the truth) or a Knave (always lies). Their statements:
- A: "B is a knave."
- B: "A and C are of the same type."
- C: "A is a knight."

We must find the unique consistent assignment and show that the alternatives are ruled out.

Step 1: Suppose A is a Knight. Then A's statement is true, so "B is a knave" is true → B is a Knave. Since B is a Knave, B's statement is false. B said "A and C are of the same type"; its negation is that A and C are NOT of the same type. A is a Knight, so C must be a Knave. Now examine C: C is a Knave, so C's statement "A is a knight" must be false, which would mean A is NOT a knight (i.e., A is a Knave). But we assumed A is a Knight — contradiction. Therefore A cannot be a Knight. So A must be a Knave.

Step 2: Suppose A is a Knave. Then A's statement "B is a knave" is false, so B is NOT a knave → B is a Knight. Since B is a Knight, B's statement "A and C are of the same type" is true. A is a Knave, so for them to be the same type, C must also be a Knave. Now verify C: C is a Knave, so C's statement "A is a knight" must be false, meaning A is not a knight → A is a Knave. This is consistent with our assumption.

Step 3: Check all three statements under A=Knave, B=Knight, C=Knave:
- A (Knave) says "B is a knave" → false, because B is a Knight. ✓ (a knave lies)
- B (Knight) says "A and C are of the same type" → A and C are both Knaves, so same type → true. ✓
- C (Knave) says "A is a knight" → false, since A is a Knave. ✓
All consistent.

Step 4: Rule out remaining possibilities explicitly. The 8 possible assignments: We already showed A cannot be a Knight (Case 1 led to contradiction), so A must be Knave. Given A=Knave, B must be Knight (otherwise if B were Knave, A's false statement "B is knave" would have to be false meaning B is Knight — contradiction, so B=Knight forced). Given B=Knight, C must equal A's type = Knave. The assignment is forced uniquely; no other combination satisfies all three statements. Hence the only solution is A=Knave, B=Knight, C=Knave.

【答案】A 是无赖，B 是骑士，C 是无赖。

推理：假设 A 是骑士，则 A 说真话→B 是无赖；B 是无赖则其话为假→"A 与 C 同类"为假，即 A、C 不同类，故 C 是无赖；但 C 是无赖则其话"A 是骑士"为假→A 不是骑士，与假设矛盾。故 A 必为无赖。A 是无赖→其话"B 是无赖"为假→B 是骑士；B 是骑士→其话为真→A 与 C 同类，A 是无赖故 C 也是无赖；验证 C（无赖）说"A 是骑士"为假，成立。逐一排除后唯一自洽解：A 无赖、B 骑士、C 无赖。
