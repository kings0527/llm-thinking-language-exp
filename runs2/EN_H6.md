# 解题答案（顺序：H3, H1, H5, H2, H6, H4）

## H3

【思考】
We are asked to compare two sentences:
(A) He didn't reply.
(B) He didn't bother to reply.

(a) Literal propositional content: Both sentences assert the same proposition — that the act of replying did not occur. Formally, ¬Reply(he). There is no difference in the truth-conditional content. The difference lies entirely in the speaker's stance/attitude. Sentence (A) is neutral: it merely reports that no reply happened. Sentence (B), by adding "bother to", expresses the speaker's evaluation: the speaker regards the reply as a small, trivial effort that the person could easily have made, and implies criticism, irritation, or disappointment that the person did not make even that small effort.

(b) Presupposition and implicature of "bother to": The construction "bother to V" presupposes that V is a minor, easily performable action (a small courtesy or effort) that one would normally be expected to do, and that doing it would not be a burden. It presupposes the availability and low cost of the action. The conversational implicature (Gricean) is that the speaker is annoyed or disapproving: by saying he "didn't bother", the speaker conveys that the person was lazy, indifferent, or rude in failing to perform a trivial expected action. The implicature is "he could have easily replied, and he should have, but he chose not to out of carelessness."

(c) Why it cannot describe something the speaker thinks "should not be expected to happen": Because "bother to V" presupposes V is a small, routine, expected courtesy. If the speaker believes V is not at all to be expected (extraordinary, heroic, or beyond normal expectation), then the criticism embedded in "didn't bother" loses its basis — there is no small expected effort that was shirked. Example: "He didn't bother to fly to the moon to deliver my letter." Flying to the moon is not a trivial expected courtesy, so the sentence is absurd/non-natural; one would never criticize someone for "not bothering" to do something wildly disproportionate. Another example: "He didn't bother to save the drowning child" — saving a life is a major, not a trivial, act; using "bother" here misrepresents the action as a casual courtesy and sounds inappropriate. The construction requires the action to be a minor expected one.

【答案】
(a) 两句话的字面命题内容没有差别，都表示"他没有回复"。差别在于说话人立场：(A) 是中性陈述；(B) 加上 "bother to" 后带有说话人的负面评价，暗示回复是件轻而易举、本应做到的小事，说话人对此感到不满、失望或批评。
(b) 加上 "bother to" 预设了"回复"是一件微不足道、本应做且毫不费力的小礼节/小举动；它隐含（implicate）了说话人的恼火与指责——此人明明可以轻松回复、也本该回复，却因懒惰或冷漠而没做。
(c) 因为 "bother to V" 预设 V 是一件轻微、常规、本可期待的小事；若说话人认为某事根本不该被期待（如极端或非凡之事），则其中"本可轻松做到却偷懒"的指责就失去了基础。例如 "He didn't bother to fly to the moon to deliver my letter" 或 "He didn't bother to save the drowning child" 都不自然——登月或救人都不是可随意期待的小礼节，用 "bother" 来形容显得荒谬或不恰当。

## H1

【思考】
The sentence is from Mencius: "王顾左右而言他" (The king looked around at his attendants and spoke of other things).

(a) Meaning of "他": In this context, "他" means "other things / something else" (别的、其他的事). The king, being embarrassed or unwilling to continue the topic, turned his head to look left and right at his attendants and changed the subject to speak about other matters. So "他" here is a nominal meaning "other (things)."

(b) Why it cannot be the modern third-person pronoun "he/him": In pre-Qin Chinese (先秦汉语), "他" was not used as a third-person pronoun at all. The third-person pronouns in classical Chinese were 之, 其, and 彼. The word "他" (originally written 佗 or 它) meant "other, another, different" — it is cognate with 它 ("other"). It only gradually grammaticalized into the third-person pronoun "he/him" in later (medieval and modern) Chinese. Therefore, reading "他" as the modern personal pronoun is an anachronism; the text predates that usage.

(c) Concrete偏差 if read as third-person pronoun: If one mistakenly interprets "他" as "him/he", the sentence would be parsed as "王顾左右而言他" = "the king looked left and right and spoke TO him" — i.e., the king spoke to some third party. This completely changes the meaning from "the king changed the subject / spoke of other things (evading the topic)" to "the king spoke to someone." The original point of the anecdote — that the king, cornered by Mencius's questioning, evaded the issue by diverting to another topic — is lost. The phrase would no longer convey the king's evasion but rather a directional act of speaking to a person, which is a semantic distortion. In pre-Qin usage, "他" as "other" is well attested (e.g., "他日" = another day; "他国" = another state), confirming the "other things" reading.

【答案】
(a) "他" 意思是"别的、其他（的事）"，全句意为：大王环顾左右臣子，说起了别的话题（转移了话题）。
(b) 不能理解为现代汉语第三人称代词，因为在先秦汉语中 "他" 并非人称代词；当时第三人称用 "之、其、彼"。"他"（本字或作 "佗/它"）原义是"别的、其他的"，直到中古以后才虚化为第三人称代词。因此用现代人称去读是时代误置。
(c) 若按"第三人称代词"理解，整句会变成"大王环顾左右，对他（某人）说话"，意思从"王顾左右而言他=王转移话题、避而不答"完全扭曲为"王对某人说话"，丢失了原文中"王被问住后故意岔开话题"的语义。结合先秦 "他" 表"其他"的用法（如"他日""他国"），此处只能作"别的事"解。

## H5

【思考】
The sentence: "他谁都不认识。"

(a) Possible readings:
Reading 1 (他 as subject): "He does not know anyone." = 他不认识任何人. Here "他" is the subject of "认识", and "谁" is an indefinite universal (任指) meaning "anyone / everyone" bound by the "谁…都" construction under negation. The negation scopes over the universal: for all x, he does not know x.
Reading 2 (他 as object / topic): "No one knows him." = 没有人认识他. Here "他" is topicalized as the object of "认识", and "谁" is the subject (also 任指, "anyone") of the clause: [as for him], anyone does not know him. This reading arises from Chinese topic-prominence, where "他" can be fronted as a topic that is semantically the object.
In both readings, "谁" is 任指 (free/universal indefinite, 'anyone'), not interrogative and not 虚指 (which would be a non-specific "someone", e.g., "谁来了" with vague reference).

(b) Syntactic/morphological root of ambiguity: Chinese lacks morphological case marking and is a topic-prominent language. The NP "他" can be parsed either as the subject of the clause or as a fronted topic that is the object of the verb "认识". Together with the "谁…都" universal quantifier construction and the sentence-initial negation scope, the same linear string supports two distinct grammatical relations for "他". The ambiguity is thus structural: it comes from the dual availability of subject vs. object/topic assignment for the initial NP, with no morphological cue to disambiguate.

(c) Comparison with "他谁都认识": This affirmative sentence is ALSO ambiguous between "He knows everyone" (他=subject) and "Everyone knows him" (他=object/topic). However, the degree of ambiguity is NOT identical to the negative version. In the negative "他谁都不认识", the subject reading (他不认识任何人) is strongly the unmarked / default interpretation; the object reading (谁都不认识他) is possible but requires a supportive context where "他" is clearly a topic. In the affirmative "他谁都认识", the two readings are more nearly balanced in availability — both "he knows everyone" and "everyone knows him" come readily, because the affirmative lacks the strong pull that negation gives to the subject-wide-scope reading. So while both sentences are ambiguous, the negative one is weighted toward the subject reading, whereas the affirmative shows a more symmetric (and thus arguably more pronounced) ambiguity.

【答案】
(a) 两种理解：
- 理解一（"他"作主语）："他谁都不认识"= 他不认识任何人。"谁"是任指（任何人），在否定句中受"谁…都"约束，否定辖域覆盖全句。
- 理解二（"他"作话题/宾语）："他谁都不认识"= 没有人认识他。"他"被话题化，是动词"认识"的宾语；"谁"是任指主语（任何人）。
两种理解中"谁"都是任指用法（非疑问、非虚指）。
(b) 歧义的句法/形态根源：汉语缺乏形态格标记，且是话题优先语言。句首的"他"既可分析为主语，也可分析为前置的话题（实为动词"认识"的宾语）；再结合"谁…都"全称量化与句首否定辖域，同一线性串就支持了"他"的两种不同语法关系，造成结构歧义。
(c) 与"他谁都认识"相比，歧义程度并不完全相同。肯定句"他谁都认识"同样有"他认识所有人"和"所有人都认识他"两种读法，且两种读法可用性更均衡；而否定句"他谁都不认识"中"他不认识任何人"（主语读法）是明显更无标记、更主导的理解，"没有人认识他"（宾语/话题读法）需依赖语境。因此否定句偏向主语解读，肯定句的两种解读更对称、歧义相对更突出。

## H2

【思考】
We must count 7-digit numbers using digits 1,2,3,4,5,6,7 exactly once each (all distinct), that are divisible by 11.

Divisibility by 11 test: A number is divisible by 11 iff the alternating sum of its digits is a multiple of 11. For a 7-digit number with digits d1 d2 d3 d4 d5 d6 d7 (positions counted from the left), let
O = d1 + d3 + d5 + d7  (sum of digits in odd positions; there are 4 such positions)
E = d2 + d4 + d6        (sum of digits in even positions; there are 3 such positions)
The number is divisible by 11 iff (O − E) ≡ 0 (mod 11).

Total sum of all digits: 1+2+3+4+5+6+7 = 28. So O + E = 28, hence E = 28 − O.
Then O − E = O − (28 − O) = 2O − 28.
We need 2O − 28 ≡ 0 (mod 11) → 2O ≡ 28 (mod 11). Since 28 ≡ 6 (mod 11), we have 2O ≡ 6 (mod 11).
The multiplicative inverse of 2 modulo 11 is 6 (because 2·6 = 12 ≡ 1 mod 11). Multiply both sides by 6: O ≡ 6·6 = 36 ≡ 3 (mod 11). So O ≡ 3 (mod 11).

Now determine the possible range of O. O is the sum of 4 distinct digits chosen from {1,…,7}. Minimum O = 1+2+3+4 = 10. Maximum O = 4+5+6+7 = 22. So 10 ≤ O ≤ 22.
Within this range, values congruent to 3 mod 11 are: 3 (too small, <10), 14, and 25 (too large, >22). Thus O must equal 14.
Consequently E = 28 − 14 = 14, and O − E = 0, which is divisible by 11. (We also check the other multiples: O − E = ±11 would require O = 19.5 or 8.5, not integers, so impossible.)

So the problem reduces to: choose 4 digits from {1,…,7} whose sum is 14 (these occupy the odd positions); the remaining 3 digits occupy the even positions.

Enumerate all 4-element subsets summing to 14:
- {1,2,4,7} = 14
- {1,2,5,6} = 14
- {1,3,4,6} = 14
- {2,3,4,5} = 14
No others exist (verified by complement: the 3-element even-position sets summing to 14 are {3,5,6}, {3,4,7}, {2,5,7}, {1,6,7}, exactly the complements of the four sets above). So there are 4 such subsets.

For each valid choice of the 4 odd-position digits:
- The 4 chosen digits can be arranged among the 4 odd positions in 4! = 24 ways.
- The remaining 3 digits can be arranged among the 3 even positions in 3! = 6 ways.
Total per subset = 24 × 6 = 144.
With 4 subsets, total = 4 × 144 = 576.

Therefore, there are 576 such seven-digit numbers.

【答案】
能被 11 整除的七位数共有 **576** 个。

推导：被 11 整除的判别法是奇数位数字和与偶数位数字和之差为 11 的倍数。设奇数位（第1、3、5、7位，共4个）数字和为 O，偶数位（第2、4、6位，共3个）数字和为 E。所有数字 1+2+…+7=28，故 O+E=28。需 O−E ≡ 0 (mod 11)，即 2O−28 ≡ 0 (mod 11) → 2O ≡ 6 (mod 11) → O ≡ 3 (mod 11)。O 为4个不同数字之和，范围在 10 到 22 之间，故唯一可能为 O=14，从而 E=14。

从 {1,…,7} 中取4个数字和为14的子集共有4个：{1,2,4,7}、{1,2,5,6}、{1,3,4,6}、{2,3,4,5}。每个子集的4个数字排入奇数位有 4! 种，剩余3个数字排入偶数位有 3! 种。故总数 = 4 × 4! × 3! = 4 × 24 × 6 = 576。

## H6

【思考】
We evaluate six learner sentences for naturalness/correctness, identify error types, and give corrections.

(1) I'm looking forward to meet you.
Error: "look forward to" — here "to" is a preposition, not part of an infinitive, so it must be followed by a gerund. Correction: "I'm looking forward to meeting you."

(2) She suggested me to take the train.
Error: The verb "suggest" does not take the pattern "suggest + object + infinitive". It takes either a gerund ("suggest doing") or a that-clause ("suggest that someone do"). Correction: "She suggested (that) I take the train" or "She suggested taking the train." (Note: "advise/recommend someone to do" exists, but "suggest" does not.)

(3) Despite of the rain, we went out.
Error: "despite" is already a preposition and is NOT followed by "of". The phrase "in spite of" uses "of", but "despite" does not. Correction: "Despite the rain, we went out" (or "In spite of the rain, we went out").

(4) He's capable to do it.
Error: The adjective "capable" is followed by "of + gerund", not by an infinitive. Correction: "He's capable of doing it."

(5) I would rather stay than to go.
Error: The correlative "would rather … than …" takes bare infinitives (without "to") after both parts. The "to" after "than" is ungrammatical. Correction: "I would rather stay than go."

(6) It's worth to try.
Error: The adjective "worth" is followed by a gerund, not an infinitive. Correction: "It's worth trying" (or "It's worth a try").

【答案】
(1) 错误。"look forward to" 中 to 是介词，须接动名词。应改为：I'm looking forward to meeting you.
(2) 错误。"suggest" 不能接 "宾语 + 不定式"，应接动名词或 that 从句。应改为：She suggested (that) I take the train. 或 She suggested taking the train.
(3) 错误。"despite" 是介词，后面不接 of（in spite of 才接 of）。应改为：Despite the rain, we went out. 或 In spite of the rain, we went out.
(4) 错误。"capable" 后接 of + 动名词，不接不定式。应改为：He's capable of doing it.
(5) 错误。"would rather … than …" 两端都用不带 to 的动词原形。应改为：I would rather stay than go.
(6) 错误。"worth" 后接动名词，不接不定式。应改为：It's worth trying. 或 It's worth a try.

## H4

【思考】
There are three people A, B, C. Each is either a Knight (always tells the truth) or a Knave (always lies).
Statements:
- A: "B is a knave."
- B: "A and C are of the same type."
- C: "A is a knight."

We must find the unique consistent assignment and rule out the alternatives.

Consider two cases for A.

Case 1: A is a Knight.
Then A's statement is true → "B is a knave" is true → B is a knave.
Since B is a knave, B's statement is false. B said "A and C are of the same type." For this to be false, A and C must be of DIFFERENT types. A is a knight, so C must be a knave.
Now C is a knave, so C's statement must be false. C said "A is a knight." But A IS a knight, so C's statement is actually TRUE. This contradicts the requirement that a knave's statement be false. Therefore Case 1 is impossible.

Case 2: A is a Knave.
Then A's statement is false. A said "B is a knave," so the truth is that B is NOT a knave → B is a knight.
Since B is a knight, B's statement is true. B said "A and C are of the same type." A is a knave, so C must also be a knave for them to be the same type.
Now check C: C is a knave, so C's statement must be false. C said "A is a knight." Since A is a knave, this statement is indeed false — consistent.
All three statements are now satisfied: A (knave) falsely calls B a knave (B is actually a knight ✓); B (knight) truthfully says A and C are the same type (both knaves ✓); C (knave) falsely calls A a knight (A is a knave ✓).

Since Case 1 leads to contradiction and Case 2 is fully consistent, the unique solution is: A = knave, B = knight, C = knave.

【答案】
唯一一致的身份分配是：**A 是无赖，B 是骑士，C 是无赖**。

推理（排除法）：
- 假设 A 是骑士：则 A 说真话 → "B 是无赖"为真 → B 是无赖。B 说谎 → "A 和 C 同类"为假 → A、C 不同类；A 是骑士，故 C 是无赖。但 C 是无赖必须说假话，而 C 说"A 是骑士"却是真话，矛盾。故 A 不可能是骑士。
- 因此 A 必为无赖：A 说谎 → "B 是无赖"为假 → B 是骑士。B 说真话 → "A 和 C 同类"为真；A 是无赖，故 C 也是无赖。验证 C：C 是无赖应说假话，C 说"A 是骑士"确为假（A 是无赖），一致。
所有陈述均满足，且已排除 A 为骑士的可能，故答案唯一。
