## H3

【思考】
(A) "He didn't reply." (B) "He didn't bother to reply."
(a) Literal propositional content: Both sentences entail the proposition that he did not produce a reply. Sentence (A) simply negates the event of replying. Sentence (B) negates the act of "bothering to reply," i.e., he did not expend the minimal effort required to reply; this logically entails that he did not reply (you cannot bother to do something you actually did). So at the level of truth-conditional content, (B) is at least as informative as (A); (B) entails (A), though the two are not strictly identical because (B) additionally predicates something about his willingness/effort. Speaker stance: (A) is neutral and reportive. (B) expresses the speaker's evaluation: replying was a trivial, low-cost action that the subject easily could have performed, yet the subject chose not to exert even that minimal effort, implying laziness, dismissiveness, or rudeness.
(b) Presupposition of "bother to": The construction "bother to V" presupposes that V is a small, easy, low-cost action that was, in the context, a live option and at least a reasonable expectation (something one might normally do). It also presupposes the subject had the capacity and the opportunity to perform V at minimal cost. Implicature (conversational): The speaker implies that the subject was indifferent, lazy, or rude; that the subject deliberately withheld even trivial effort; and that the failure to act reflects negatively on the subject's attitude. This is a Gricean implicature arising because saying "didn't bother" rather than merely "didn't" conversationally suggests the speaker holds the subject blameworthy.
(c) "He didn't bother to reply" cannot felicitously describe an action the speaker thinks should not even be expected. Because "bother to" presupposes the action was a minor, expected, easily performed task, it is anomalous when applied to an action that is extraordinary, impossible, or not contextually expected of the subject. Example: "The one-year-old didn't bother to file his tax return." Filing a tax return is not an expected or trivial task for a toddler, so the presupposition fails and the sentence is pragmatically infelicitous (absurd). Another example: "The rock didn't bother to roll down the hill" — a rock has no agency and rolling is not a contextually expected intentional act, so "bother to" is inappropriate. Hence the construction requires a baseline expectation that the subject would (easily) perform the action.

【答案】
(a) 两句话在真值命题内容上基本一致——都断言"他没有回复"，且(B)蕴含(A)。但说话人立场不同：(A)是中性陈述；(B)通过 bother to 表达了对主语的负面评价，暗示回复是轻而易举的小事，而主语却连这点 effort 都不愿付出，显得怠慢、无礼或冷漠。
(b) 加上 bother to 后，这句话预设（presuppose）：回复是一件成本低、轻而易举、且在语境中本可被合理期待发生的小事。它隐含（implicate）：主语是懒惰/漠不关心/无礼的，是故意连最微小的 effort 都不愿付出，从而应受责备。
(c) 因为 bother to 预设动作是"轻微、易被期待、本可轻松完成"的；若说话人认为该动作"本就不该期待发生"，预设即落空，句子便不自然。例：*The one-year-old didn't bother to file his tax return.（让一岁小孩报税不是可期待的小事，用 bother to 荒谬）。又如 *The rock didn't bother to roll down the hill.（石头无施事能力，也不适用）。

## H1

【思考】
The sentence "Wang gu zuo you er yan ta" (The king looked left and right and spoke of other things) appears in the Mencius (Mengzi). The phrase "yan ta" means "speak of other things / speak of something else."
(a) Meaning of "ta": In pre-Qin Chinese, "ta" meant "other, another, different" (gloss: bie de / qi ta). So "yan ta" = "spoke of other matters," i.e., the king changed the subject to avoid the embarrassing question.
(b) Why it cannot be the modern third-person pronoun "he/him": The modern third-person pronoun reading of "ta" (referring to a person) did not exist in the classical language. In Old Chinese, third-person reference was expressed by forms such as "zhi" (used in object position), "qi" (used in possessive/genitive position), and occasionally "jue" or "bi." The character "ta" (also written with an alternate graph) originally meant "other / another" and was used as an adjectival demonstrative meaning "different." The grammaticalization of "ta" into a third-person personal pronoun is a much later development, emerging in the medieval and vernacular periods (roughly from the Six Dynasties onward, especially under the influence of Buddhist sutra translation and later baihua literature). Therefore a pre-Qin text like the Mencius cannot use "ta" as a personal pronoun.
(c) If one mistakenly reads "ta" as the third-person pronoun "him," the sentence would be parsed as "The king looked left and right and spoke about him (that other person)." This wrongly introduces a specific referent (a third party) who is being talked about, changing the meaning from "the king evaded the issue by changing the topic to other matters" into "the king mentioned some other man." This concrete distortion shows the anachronistic error of imposing modern grammar on a classical text.

【答案】
(a) "他"在句中意为"别的、其他的（事情）"，"言他"即"说别的事/岔开话题"。
(b) 不能理解为现代汉语第三人称代词"他（他/她）"，因为在先秦汉语中"他"并无人称代词用法。当时第三人称用"之"（宾语）、"其"（定语）等表示；"他"（早期亦作"佗"）本义就是"别的、其他的"，作形容词性指示语。把"他"当作人称代词是六朝以后、尤其是近代白话才有的语法化结果，孟子时代尚无此用法。
(c) 若按"第三人称代词"理解，整句会被误读为"王环顾左右，说起他（那个别人）来"，凭空引入一个被谈论的第三者，把原意"王因尴尬而转移话题、说别的事"歪曲成"王提到了某个具体的人"。这正是以现代语法强加于古文所造成的误读。

## H5

【思考】
Consider the sentence "Ta shei dou bu ren shi" (literally: he / who / all / not / know).
(a) Two readings:
Reading 1 (ta as subject): ta [topic/subject] shei [object] dou bu ren shi. Interpretation: "He does not know anyone" (for every person x, he does not know x). Here "shei" is a free/universal indefinite (renzhi), meaning "anyone / everybody" under the scope of negation and the universal marker "dou."
Reading 2 (ta as object): shei [topic/subject] dou bu ren shi ta [object]. Interpretation: "Nobody knows him" (for every person x, x does not know him). Here again "shei" is a free/universal indefinite (meaning "everyone"), and "ta" is the patient/object. In both readings "shei" is a free indefinite, not an interrogative (the presence of "dou" blocks the interrogative reading) and not a virtual indefinite (the virtual use typically appears in affirmative existential contexts such as "ta hao xiang ren shi shei," he seems to know someone).
(b) Syntactic/morphological source of ambiguity: Chinese lacks case marking and agreement morphology, so the grammatical relation of a noun phrase is not encoded on the word itself. The sentence contains two NPs ("ta," "shei") and one transitive verb ("ren shi," know/recognize). The universal free indefinite "shei" must precede "dou," forcing "shei" into the topic position; but "ta" can either be a separate topic (subject, Reading 1) or remain the post-verbal object (Reading 2). Because Chinese allows both subject-topic and object-topic (fronting) structures with no morphological distinction, the structure [ta_i [shei_j dou bu ren shi t_i]] (ta subject) and [shei_j dou bu ren shi ta_i] (ta object) are both available. The ambiguity thus originates in the free assignment of subject/object roles to "ta" under a topic–comment syntax with no case marking.
(c) Comparison with "Ta shei dou ren shi" (He knows everyone): The two sentences are NOT equally ambiguous. The negative sentence "Ta shei dou bu ren shi" robustly yields both readings. The positive "Ta shei dou ren shi" overwhelmingly yields only "He knows everyone" (ta subject reading); the "Everyone knows him" (ta object) reading is strongly dispreferred or effectively blocked. Reason: In the positive, linear order and the subject-topic preference make the initial "ta" the natural subject, and "knowing everyone" is a natural property predicated of "ta"; the object-topic reading is much less accessible without dislocation. With negation, however, the negative operator interacts with the universal "shei dou" to license the object-topic scope more readily, and pragmatically both readings (he knows nobody / nobody knows him) are equally plausible, so the negative sentence is the more thoroughly ambiguous of the two.

【答案】
(a) "他谁都不认识"有两种理解：
理解1（他是主语）："他[主语]谁[宾语]都不认识" → 他谁也不认识／他不认识任何人。句中"谁"是任指（意为"任何人/每个人"，在否定与"都"的统括之下）。
理解2（他是宾语）："谁[主语]都不认识他[宾语]" → 谁也不认识他／没有人认识他。句中"谁"仍是任指（"每个人"），"他"作受事宾语。两种理解里"谁"都是任指，不是疑问（"都"排除了疑问读法），也不是虚指（虚指多用于"他好像认识谁"这类肯定存在句）。
(b) 歧义的句法/形态根源：汉语缺乏格标记与一致关系形态，名词的语法角色不体现在词形上。句中两个 NP（他、谁）与一个及物动词"认识"的配位不固定；任指"谁"必须居于"都"前，被迫进入话题位，而"他"既可作独立话题（主语，理解1），也可留在动词后作宾语（理解2）。在话题—述评结构且无格标记的条件下，[他_i [谁_j 都不认识 t_i]]（他主）与[谁_j 都不认识 他_i]（他宾）两种结构都合法，从而产生歧义。
(c) "他谁都认识"的歧义程度与前者不同，不等同。"他谁都不认识"（否定）两种理解都很自然、歧义充分；而"他谁都认识"（肯定）几乎只取"他认识所有人"（他主语）这一读，"所有人都认识他"（他宾语）那一种读被强烈抑制。原因在于：肯定句中线性语序与主语话题倾向使句首的"他"自然被分析为主语，"认识所有人"是赋予"他"的自然属性；而否定词与"谁都"的辖域互动更易催生宾语话题读法，且"他不认识任何人／任何人不认识他"两种语义在语用上同样合理，故否定句歧义更充分。

## H2

【思考】
We form 7-digit numbers using digits 1,2,3,4,5,6,7 each exactly once. Count those divisible by 11.
Divisibility by 11 rule: A number is divisible by 11 iff the alternating sum of its digits (sum of digits in odd positions minus sum of digits in even positions) is a multiple of 11.
Let positions be d1 d2 d3 d4 d5 d6 d7 (from left). Odd positions: {1,3,5,7} (4 digits); even positions: {2,4,6} (3 digits). Let O = sum of odd-position digits, E = sum of even-position digits.
Total O + E = 1+2+3+4+5+6+7 = 28.
Alternating sum S = O − E = O − (28 − O) = 2O − 28.
For divisibility by 11, S ≡ 0 (mod 11), i.e., 2O − 28 ∈ {…,−22,−11,0,11,22,…}.
Solve 2O ≡ 28 (mod 11). 28 ≡ 6 (mod 11), so 2O ≡ 6 (mod 11). Multiply by inverse of 2 mod 11, which is 6 (since 2·6=12≡1). O ≡ 6·6 = 36 ≡ 3 (mod 11).
O is the sum of 4 distinct digits from 1..7. Minimum O = 1+2+3+4 = 10; maximum O = 4+5+6+7 = 22. So O ∈ [10,22]. Values ≡ 3 (mod 11) in this range: only 14 (since 3 is too small, 25 too large). Thus O = 14 exactly, giving S = 0.
Equivalently checking multiples: 2O−28 = 0 → O=14 ✓; =11 → O=19.5 ✗; =−11 → O=8.5 ✗; =22 → O=25 ✗; =−22 → O=3 ✗. Only O=14 works.
Now E = 28 − 14 = 14, so the 3 even-position digits must also sum to 14.
Count the 4-element subsets of {1,2,3,4,5,6,7} summing to 14. Enumerate a<b<c<d, a+b+c+d=14:
- a=1: b+c+d=13.
  b=2: c+d=11 → (c,d)=(4,7),(5,6) → {1,2,4,7}, {1,2,5,6}.
  b=3: c+d=10 → (c,d)=(4,6) → {1,3,4,6}.
  b=4: c+d=9 → none (c≥5 ⇒ d≤4, impossible).
- a=2: b+c+d=12.
  b=3: c+d=9 → (c,d)=(4,5) → {2,3,4,5}.
  b=4: c+d=8 → none.
- a=3: b+c+d=11, min 4+5+6=15 > 11 → none.
So exactly 4 subsets: {1,2,4,7}, {1,2,5,6}, {1,3,4,6}, {2,3,4,5}. Their complements each sum to 14, as required.
For each such choice of the 4 odd-position digits: arrange them in the 4 odd positions in 4! = 24 ways, and arrange the 3 remaining digits in the 3 even positions in 3! = 6 ways. So per subset: 24 × 6 = 144 numbers.
Total = 4 × 144 = 576.
Therefore there are 576 such seven-digit numbers divisible by 11.

【答案】
按 11 的整除判别法：奇数位数字和与偶数位数字和之差为 11 的倍数即可。
设奇数位（第1、3、5、7位，共4个数字）之和为 O，偶数位（第2、4、6位，共3个数字）之和为 E。1+2+…+7 = 28，故 O+E=28，且差 S = O−E = 2O−28。要求 S ≡ 0 (mod 11)。
由 2O−28 ≡ 0 得 2O ≡ 28 ≡ 6 (mod 11)；2 在模 11 下的逆元为 6，故 O ≡ 36 ≡ 3 (mod 11)。O 为 4 个相异数字之和，范围 10≤O≤22，其中 ≡3(mod11) 的仅 O=14。于是 O=14，E=14。
枚举 {1..7} 中和为 14 的 4 元子集：{1,2,4,7}、{1,2,5,6}、{1,3,4,6}、{2,3,4,5}，共 4 组（其补集和亦为 14）。
每组中，4 个奇数位数字有 4! 种排法，3 个偶数位数字有 3! 种排法，故每组得 4!×3! = 24×6 = 144 个数。
总数 = 4 × 144 = 576。
答：能被 11 整除的七位数共有 **576** 个。

## H6

【思考】
Evaluate each sentence for naturalness/correctness, identify error type, and give corrected form.
(1) "I'm looking forward to meet you." — Error: "look forward to" takes a gerund, not an infinitive, because "to" here is a preposition, not part of an infinitive. Correction: "I'm looking forward to meeting you."
(2) "She suggested me to take the train." — Error: "suggest" does not take the pattern "suggest + indirect object + to-infinitive." Correct patterns: "suggest (that) someone (should) do something" or "suggest doing something." Correction: "She suggested (that) I take the train" or "She suggested taking the train."
(3) "Despite of the rain, we went out." — Error: "despite" is a preposition and is not followed by "of"; "despite of" is ungrammatical. Correction: "Despite the rain, we went out." (Alternatively "In spite of the rain.")
(4) "He's capable to do it." — Error: "capable" is followed by "of + gerund," not an infinitive. Correction: "He's capable of doing it."
(5) "I would rather stay than to go." — Error: after "would rather … than," parallel bare infinitives are required; the "to" before "go" is incorrect. Correction: "I would rather stay than go."
(6) "It's worth to try." — Error: "worth" is followed by a gerund, not an infinitive. Correction: "It's worth trying." (If one wants an infinitive, use "worthwhile": "It's worthwhile to try.")

【答案】
(1) 不自然。错误类型：介词 to 后误用不定式（look forward to 中 to 是介词，须接动名词）。正确：I'm looking forward to meeting you.
(2) 不正确。错误类型：suggest 不能接"间接宾语 + to 不定式"（无 suggest sb to do 结构）。正确：She suggested (that) I take the train. 或 She suggested taking the train.
(3) 不正确。错误类型：despite 是介词，不能加 of（"despite of" 错误）。正确：Despite the rain, we went out. （或 In spite of the rain）
(4) 不正确。错误类型：capable 须接 of + 动名词，不能接不定式。正确：He's capable of doing it.
(5) 不正确。错误类型：would rather … than … 后接并列的动词原形（裸不定式），than 后不应有 to。正确：I would rather stay than go.
(6) 不正确。错误类型：worth 后接动名词，不接不定式。正确：It's worth trying. （若要用不定式，应改为 worthwhile：It's worthwhile to try.）

## H4

【思考】
Three people A, B, C; each is a knight (always tells truth) or a knave (always lies).
Statements:
A: "B is a knave."
B: "A and C are of the same type."
C: "A is a knight."
We must find the unique assignment and exclude all others.
Begin with C's statement, "A is a knight."
Suppose C is a knight (truth-teller). Then C's statement is true, so A is a knight. Since A is a knight, A's statement "B is a knave" is true, so B is a knave. Since B is a knave, B's statement "A and C are of the same type" must be false. But A (knight) and C (knight) are indeed of the same type, making B's statement true — contradiction (a knave cannot utter a truth). Therefore C cannot be a knight; C must be a knave.
Since C is a knave, C's statement "A is a knight" is false, so A is a knave.
Since A is a knave, A's statement "B is a knave" is false, so B is a knight.
Now verify B: B is a knight, so B's statement "A and C are of the same type" must be true. A is a knave and C is a knave — they are the same type — so the statement is true, consistent.
Thus the only consistent assignment is: A = knave, B = knight, C = knave.
Exclusion check of the eight possibilities: The deduction above forced C = knave (any C = knight leads to contradiction), which forced A = knave, which forced B = knight. Hence all other 7 combinations are ruled out: C cannot be knight (shown); with C knave, A must be knave (if A were knight, C's false statement "A is knight" would be true, contradiction); with A knave, B must be knight (if B were knave, A's false statement "B is knave" would be true, contradiction). Only one assignment remains.

【答案】
设骑士（说真话）为 T，无赖（说假话）为 F。
从 C 的话入手："A 是骑士。"
- 若 C 是骑士，则" A 是骑士"为真 → A 是骑士。A 说" B 是无赖"为真 → B 是无赖。B 说" A 和 C 同类"应为假；但 A(骑士)与 C(骑士)确为同类，使 B 的话为真，矛盾。故 C 不可能是骑士，C 必为无赖。
- C 是无赖，则" A 是骑士"为假 → A 是无赖。
- A 是无赖，则" B 是无赖"为假 → B 是骑士。
- 验证 B：B 是骑士，" A 和 C 同类"须为真；A(无赖)与 C(无赖)同类，成立。
排除说明：C 若为骑士必矛盾，故 C=无赖；C 无赖则 A 必为无赖（否则 C 的假话变真）；A 无赖则 B 必为骑士（否则 A 的假话变真）。其余 7 种组合均被排除。
结论：**A 是无赖，B 是骑士，C 是无赖。**
