## H3

【思考】
(a) Compare the literal propositional content and the speaker stance of (A) "He didn't reply." and (B) "He didn't bother to reply."
- Step 1: Identify the truth-conditional content. Both sentences assert that the event of "his replying" did not take place. The core proposition — "He did not reply" — is identical in both; they have the same truth conditions (if he replied, both are false; if he did not, both are true). So at the level of literal proposition there is no difference.
- Step 2: Identify the difference in speaker stance/attitude. Sentence (A) is a neutral, objective report of a fact. Sentence (B) adds the phrase "bother to," which adds an evaluative layer. The speaker of (B) frames the non-reply as a failure to expend even a small amount of effort, conveying that replying was an easy, expected courtesy and that the subject's omission reflects laziness, disregard, or lack of consideration. Thus (B) expresses criticism or disappointment that (A) does not.

(b) Analyze presupposition and implicature introduced by "bother to."
- Step 1: "bother to V" conventionally presupposes that V is a low-cost, easy action, that the subject was able to perform it, and that performing V was (at least socially) expected or appropriate in the circumstances.
- Step 2: Under negation, "He didn't bother to reply" retains this presupposition: replying was an easy, warranted action he could have performed. The presupposed background is that replying was a minor, expected courtesy.
- Step 3: The conversational (here largely conventionalized) implicature is that the subject was lazy, inconsiderate, or deliberately dismissive — he could easily have replied but chose not to care enough to do so. The sentence thereby casts blame on the subject's attitude, not merely reports a fact.

(c) Explain why "He didn't bother to reply" cannot describe an event the speaker thinks was not to be expected anyway.
- Step 1: Because "bother to V" presupposes that V was expected and within the subject's easy reach, using the phrase imposes an expectation that the subject should/would perform V.
- Step 2: If the speaker believes the reply "shouldn't have been expected anyway" (e.g., the subject is incapable or under no obligation to reply), the presupposition is violated, making the utterance infelicitous/absurd.
- Step 3: Example — "My dead grandfather didn't bother to call me." Since the grandfather is dead, calling was never to be expected; saying he "didn't bother" wrongly implies he could and should have called, which is nonsensical. Another example: "The baby didn't bother to reply to my letter" — a baby cannot reply, so "didn't bother" absurdly presupposes an expectation and ability to reply.

【答案】
(a) 两句话的**字面命题内容相同**——都断言"他没有回复"这一事实，真值条件一致。差别在**说话人立场**：(A) 是中性、客观的事实陈述；(B) 因加入 "bother to"，带有评价色彩，把"未回复"框定为"连一点小力气都不肯花"，隐含责备、失望，认为回复本应是一件轻而易举、理所应当的礼节。

(b) "bother to" **预设（presuppose）**：回复是一件成本低、容易做到、且说话人认为对方本应/理当做的事，且对方有能力做到。"didn't bother to reply" 保留这一预设（回复是易行的、应尽的小小礼节）。其**隐含（implicate）**：对方懒惰、不近人情或故意怠慢——他本可轻易回复，却懒得在乎。整句因而把指责指向对方的态度，而非仅仅陈述事实。

(c) 因为 "bother to V" 预设 V 是"理应发生、对方轻易能做"的事；若说话人认为此事"本就不该期待发生"，则该预设被破坏，句子变得不合常理。例如："My dead grandfather didn't bother to call me."（我死去的祖父居然懒得给我打电话。）祖父已故，打电话本就无从期待，说"懒得打"却荒谬地预设他本该也能打。又如 "The baby didn't bother to reply to my letter."（婴儿懒得回我的信。）婴儿根本不能回信，用 "didn't bother" 等于凭空强加了一个回信的预期与能力。

## H1

【思考】
(a) Determine the meaning of "他" in the sentence "王顾左右而言他。"
- Step 1: The sentence comes from Mencius. The context is that the king, when cornered by a difficult question, looks around at his attendants and changes the subject.
- Step 2: In pre-Qin Chinese, "他" was not a third-person personal pronoun. Its basic lexical meaning was "other, another; different" (别的、其他的). So "言他" means "speak of other things / change the subject to something else." Thus "他" here means "other matters / something else."

(b) Explain why it cannot be read as the modern third-person pronoun "he/him."
- Step 1: The third-person personal pronoun in classical Chinese was expressed by "之," "其," "彼," etc., not by "他." The use of "他" as a personal pronoun for "he/him" developed much later (medieval/early modern Chinese).
- Step 2: Therefore, interpreting "他" as a personal pronoun would be an anachronism, projecting a modern grammatical function onto a word that in the Warring States period meant "other."

(c) Show the concrete distortion if read as third-person "him."
- Step 1: If "他" = "him" (a third-person referent), then "言他" would mean "spoke about him / mentioned him," requiring an antecedent male person in the context.
- Step 2: The original point — the king evading the question by changing the topic — would be lost and replaced by the claim that the king mentioned some third party, which is not what the text conveys. The sentence would wrongly import a nonexistent referent and destroy the "changing the subject" reading that is the whole point of the idiom.

【答案】
(a) "他" 在这里的意思是"**别的、其他的（事物）**"。"言他" 即"说别的事 / 把话题转到别处"，整句意为：大王环顾左右随从，说起了别的事情（即**顾左右而言他、岔开话题**）。

(b) 不能理解为现代汉语第三人称代词"他（他/她/它）"，因为**先秦汉语中 "他" 并不作人称代词**。当时表示第三人称用 "之、其、彼" 等词；"他" 的本义是"别的、其他的"。把 "他" 当作"他"这个人称代词，是把后世（中古以后才逐渐固定）的用法错误地投射到了战国时期的词汇上，属于时代错置。

(c) 若按"第三人称代词 'him'"理解，"言他" 就变成了"**说他 / 提到他**"，这就必须凭空预设语境中存在一个被提及的男性对象作为先行词；而原句的本意是"大王答不上来，于是**转移话题**"。这样理解会丢失"岔开话题"这一核心含义，并把一个原本不存在的第三者引入句中，使整句话的意思发生根本性偏差。

## H5

【思考】
(a) Enumerate the readings of "他谁都不认识" and classify the use of "谁."
- Step 1: Reading 1 — "他" is the subject (the one who recognizes), "谁" is the object within the scope of negation. The meaning is "He doesn't recognize/know anyone" = "He knows nobody." Here "谁" is a free-choice/distributive indefinite (任指), meaning "anyone/anybody," bound by "都" and negated by "不."
- Step 2: Reading 2 — "谁" is the subject (free-choice "anyone"), and "他" is the object, fronted as a topic. The underlying clause is "谁都不认识他" = "Anyone doesn't recognize him" = "Nobody knows him." Here too "谁" is 任指 ("anyone"), not interrogative.
- Step 3: In both readings "谁" is 任指 (distributive indefinite), not a question word and not 虚指 (which would be the existential "someone" as in "好像有谁在敲门").

(b) Identify the syntactic/morphological root of the ambiguity.
- Step 1: Chinese lacks morphological case marking (no nominative vs. accusative endings), so pronouns like "他" and "谁" cannot be distinguished as subject vs. object by form.
- Step 2: Word order in Chinese is topic-prominent and relatively flexible; a sentence-initial noun can be either the subject of the clause or a topicalized object.
- Step 3: Therefore "他" at the front can be parsed as the subject of "认识" (Reading 1) or as a topicalized patient/object with "谁都" serving as the subject (Reading 2). The combination of no case morphology + topic-comment flexibility licenses both parses.

(c) Compare with "他谁都认识" and assess whether the ambiguity degree is the same.
- Step 1: "他谁都认识" also has two potential readings: (i) "He knows everyone" (他 subject, 谁 object = everyone), (ii) "Everyone knows him" (谁 subject, 他 object/topic). Structurally the same ambiguity exists.
- Step 2: However, the two sentences are not equally ambiguous in practice. The negative "他谁都不认识" shows a robust, symmetric ambiguity: both "He knows nobody" and "Nobody knows him" are readily and naturally available, because the negative quantifier "谁都不 = no one" applies symmetrically to either argument.
- Step 3: In the positive "他谁都认识," the default and dominant reading is "He knows everyone"; the "Everyone knows him" reading is grammatically possible but much weaker/more dependent on special topic-comment prosody, so its ambiguity is less prominent. The asymmetry arises because the distributive object "谁都 = everyone" more naturally attaches as the object under a prominent subject "他," whereas negation makes the reversed (他-as-object) parse equally accessible.

【答案】
(a) 这句话至少有**两种理解**：
- 理解一：**他**是主语（施事），**谁**是宾语（在否定与"都"的辖域中）。意为"他**谁都不认识** = 他一个人也不认识 = 他不认识任何人（他谁也不认识）"。此处"谁"是**任指**（意为"任何人/任何人"），由"都"约束、被"不"否定。
- 理解二：**谁**是主语（任指"任何人"），**他**是宾语（话题化前置）。底层小句是"谁都不认识他" = 任何人都(不)认识他 = **没有人认识他**。此处"谁"同样是**任指**，并非疑问代词。
两种理解中"谁"都是**任指**用法（表示"任何人"），既不是疑问，也不是虚指（虚指是"有人/某人"义，如"好像有谁在敲门"）。

(b) 歧义的**句法/形态根源**：汉语**没有形态格标记**（无主格/宾格词形区别），"他""谁"这类代词无法凭形式区分主语与宾语；同时汉语是**话题优先、语序相对灵活**的语言，句首名词既可分析为小句主语，也可分析为话题化的宾语。于是句首的"他"既可读作"认识"的主语（理解一），也可读作话题化的受事宾语、而由"谁都"充当主语（理解二）。无格标记 + 话题—评论结构的可塑性，使两种分析都被允许。

(c) 与 "他谁都认识" 相比：**类型上同样存在歧义**（可读"他认识所有人"或"所有人都认识他"），但**歧义程度并不完全相同**。否定句 "他谁都不认识" 的两种理解都自然、对称，歧义很强；而肯定句 "他谁都认识" 的默认、主导理解是"他认识所有人"，"所有人都认识他"这一理解虽在语法上可能，却明显更弱、更依赖特定的话题—评论语调，因而歧义不那么突出。差别的来源在于：否定词 "不" 与 "都" 构成的 "谁都不 = 没有人" 可对称地作用于任一论元，使"他作宾语"的分析同样易得；而肯定的 "谁都 = 所有人" 更倾向于作为显性主语"他"之下的宾语，使"他作宾语"的分析相对边缘化。

## H2

【思考】
We count 7-digit numbers formed by using each of {1,2,3,4,5,6,7} exactly once, that are divisible by 11.
- Step 1: Divisibility by 11 rule. For a number with digits d1 d2 d3 d4 d5 d6 d7 (d1 most significant), the alternating sum A = d1 − d2 + d3 − d4 + d5 − d6 + d7 must be a multiple of 11 (…, −11, 0, 11, …).
- Step 2: Group positions. Let S_odd = d1 + d3 + d5 + d7 (the four positions with plus signs) and S_even = d2 + d4 + d6 (the three positions with minus signs). Then A = S_odd − S_even. Also the total digit sum is S_odd + S_even = 1+2+3+4+5+6+7 = 28.
- Step 3: Express A in one variable. A = S_odd − S_even = S_odd − (28 − S_odd) = 2·S_odd − 28. We need 2·S_odd − 28 ≡ 0 (mod 11).
- Step 4: Bound S_odd. The smallest 4 distinct digits are 1,2,3,4 summing to 10; the largest 4 are 4,5,6,7 summing to 22. So S_odd ∈ [10, 22]. Then A = 2·S_odd − 28 ∈ [−8, 16].
- Step 5: Find multiples of 11 in [−8, 16]. The only multiples of 11 in that range are 0 and 11 (note −11 is below −8 and 22 is above 16). 
  - A = 0 ⇒ 2·S_odd − 28 = 0 ⇒ S_odd = 14.
  - A = 11 ⇒ 2·S_odd = 39 ⇒ S_odd = 19.5, impossible (S_odd is an integer).
  So the only viable case is S_odd = 14, which forces S_even = 28 − 14 = 14 as well.
- Step 6: Find the 4-element subsets of {1,…,7} summing to 14. Enumerate systematically:
  - Include 1: need three more distinct digits from {2,3,4,5,6,7} summing to 13.
    * 1,2,4,7 = 14 ✓
    * 1,2,5,6 = 14 ✓
    * 1,3,4,6 = 14 ✓
    * (1,3,5,5 invalid duplicate; 1,2,3,8 no; 1,3,5,? 1+3+5=9 need 5 dup; etc.)
  - Exclude 1 (so digits from {2,3,4,5,6,7}, four of them sum to 14; min 2+3+4+5=14): 2,3,4,5 = 14 ✓
  - Any other? Check 2,3,4,5 already. 2,3,4,6=15 too big; all others exceed 14. 
  So the valid 4-subsets are exactly: {1,2,4,7}, {1,2,5,6}, {1,3,4,6}, {2,3,4,5}. That is 4 subsets. (Equivalently, the 3-element even-position subsets summing to 14 are {1,6,7},{2,5,7},{3,4,7},{3,5,6}, confirming 4.)
- Step 7: For each valid partition, assign the 4 chosen digits to the 4 odd positions (d1,d3,d5,d7) in 4! = 24 ways, and the remaining 3 digits to the 3 even positions (d2,d4,d6) in 3! = 6 ways. Since none of the digits is 0, every arrangement is a valid 7-digit number (d1 is never 0). So each partition yields 24 × 6 = 144 numbers.
- Step 8: Total = 4 partitions × 144 = 576.

Therefore, there are 576 such seven-digit numbers.

【答案】
用数字 1–7 各一次组成的七位数中，能被 11 整除的共有 **576** 个。

推导要点：
1. 被 11 整除判别法：奇数位数字和与偶数位数字和之差是 11 的倍数。设奇数位（第1、3、5、7位）数字和为 S_奇，偶数位（第2、4、6位）数字和为 S_偶，则 S_奇 − S_偶 ≡ 0 (mod 11)，且 S_奇 + S_偶 = 1+2+3+4+5+6+7 = 28。
2. 于是 S_奇 − S_偶 = 2·S_奇 − 28。S_奇 最小 10（1+2+3+4）、最大 22（4+5+6+7），故差值在 [−8, 16] 之间，其中 11 的倍数只有 0 和 11。
3. 取 0 ⇒ S_奇 = 14；取 11 ⇒ S_奇 = 19.5（非整数，舍去）。故唯一可能是 S_奇 = S_偶 = 14。
4. 从 {1,…,7} 中取 4 个数和为 14 的子集恰有 4 个：{1,2,4,7}、{1,2,5,6}、{1,3,4,6}、{2,3,4,5}。
5. 每组中 4 个奇数位数字有 4! = 24 种排法，3 个偶数位数字有 3! = 6 种排法，共 24×6 = 144 个；数字均非 0，首位不会为 0，全部有效。
6. 总计 4 × 144 = **576**。

## H6

【思考】
Evaluate each learner sentence for naturalness/correctness, identify error type, and give the corrected form.
(1) "I'm looking forward to meet you." — "look forward to" takes a noun/gerund after the preposition "to" (here "to" is a preposition, not part of an infinitive). Error type: preposition + infinitive instead of gerund. Correction: "I'm looking forward to meeting you."
(2) "She suggested me to take the train." — "suggest" does not take an indirect object + infinitive. It takes a that-clause or a gerund. Error type: wrong pattern for "suggest" (cannot say "suggest someone to do"). Correction: "She suggested (that) I take the train." or "She suggested taking the train."
(3) "Despite of the rain, we went out." — "despite" is a preposition and is NOT followed by "of"; "in spite of" is the phrase with "of." Error type: redundant "of" after "despite." Correction: "Despite the rain, we went out." (or "In spite of the rain, we went out.")
(4) "He's capable to do it." — "capable" is followed by "of" + gerund, not by an infinitive. Error type: wrong preposition/complement (capable of, not capable to). Correction: "He's capable of doing it."
(5) "I would rather stay than to go." — After "would rather … than," two parallel base (bare infinitive) verbs are used; "than" is not followed by "to." Error type: use of "to" after "than" in a "rather … than" parallel. Correction: "I would rather stay than go."
(6) "It's worth to try." — "worth" is followed by a gerund (or a noun), not by an infinitive. Error type: infinitive instead of gerund after "worth." Correction: "It's worth trying." (Note: "worthwhile" may take an infinitive — "It's worthwhile to try" — but "worth" alone does not.)

【答案】
(1) **有误。** 类型：介词后误用不定式。"look forward to" 中的 to 是介词，后接名词/动名词。正确：**I'm looking forward to meeting you.**

(2) **有误。** 类型：动词搭配错误。"suggest" 不能接 "间接宾语 + to do"，应接 that 从句或动名词。正确：**She suggested (that) I take the train.** 或 **She suggested taking the train.**

(3) **有误。** 类型：多余介词 of。"despite" 本身是介词，后面直接接名词，不与 of 连用（含 of 的是 "in spite of"）。正确：**Despite the rain, we went out.**（或 **In spite of the rain, we went out.**）

(4) **有误。** 类型：形容词搭配错误。"capable" 后接 "of + 动名词"，不接不定式。正确：**He's capable of doing it.**

(5) **有误。** 类型：平行结构错误。"would rather … than …" 后接两个并列的动词原形（不带 to）。正确：**I would rather stay than go.**

(6) **有误。** 类型：形容词后误用不定式。"worth" 后接动名词或名词，不接不定式。正确：**It's worth trying.**（若用不定式须换词：**It's worthwhile to try.**）

## H4

【思考】
A, B, C are each either a Knight (always tells truth) or a Knave (always lies).
Statements:
- A: "B is a knave."
- B: "A and C are of the same type."
- C: "A is a knight."

We must find the unique consistent assignment and rule out the others.
- Step 1: Case analysis on A.
  - Case 1: A is a Knight. Then A's statement is true ⇒ B is a Knave. Since B is a Knave, B's statement is false. B said "A and C are of the same type"; for this to be false, A and C must differ in type. A is a Knight, so C must be a Knave. Now C is a Knave, so C's statement "A is a knight" must be false. But A IS a knight, so the statement is true — contradiction (a Knave cannot utter a truth). Hence Case 1 is impossible.
  - Case 2: A is a Knave. Then A's statement "B is a knave" is false ⇒ B is NOT a knave ⇒ B is a Knight. Since B is a Knight, B's statement is true: "A and C are of the same type." A is a Knave, so C must also be a Knave. Now check C: C is a Knave, so C's statement "A is a knight" must be false. A is indeed a Knave, so "A is a knight" is false — consistent. No contradiction.
  So the only consistent assignment is A = Knave, B = Knight, C = Knave.
- Step 2: Explicitly exclude all other assignments (2^3 = 8) to be thorough:
  1. K,K,K: A says "B knave" — false, but a Knight cannot lie ⇒ fail.
  2. K,K,N: A says "B knave" — false, Knight cannot lie ⇒ fail.
  3. K,N,K: A true (B is knave ✓). B (knave) says "A,C same" — A=K,C=K so same ⇒ statement true, but knave must lie ⇒ fail.
  4. K,N,N: A true. B (knave) says "A,C same" — A=K,C=N differ ⇒ statement false, knave lies ✓. C (knave) says "A knight" — A=K so true, but knave must lie ⇒ fail.
  5. N,K,K: A (knave) says "B knave" — B=K so false, knave lies ✓. B (knight) says "A,C same" — A=N,C=K differ ⇒ statement false, but knight must tell truth ⇒ fail.
  6. N,K,N: A (knave) says "B knave" — B=K so false, knave lies ✓. B (knight) says "A,C same" — A=N,C=N same ⇒ true, knight ✓. C (knave) says "A knight" — A=N so false, knave lies ✓. WORKS.
  7. N,N,K: A (knave) says "B knave" — B=N so true, but knave must lie ⇒ fail.
  8. N,N,N: A (knave) says "B knave" — B=N so true, knave must lie ⇒ fail.
  Only assignment #6 works. Therefore A is a Knave, B is a Knight, C is a Knave.

【答案】
三人的身份为：**A 是无赖（Knave），B 是骑士（Knight），C 是无赖（Knave）。**

完整推理（排除法）：
- 假设 A 是骑士：则 A 说真话 ⇒ B 是无赖。B 是无赖 ⇒ 其语" A 与 C 同类"为假 ⇒ A、C 不同类；A 是骑士，故 C 是无赖。但 C 是无赖，其语" A 是骑士"必须为假，而 A 实际是骑士（真），矛盾。故 A 不可能是骑士。
- 故 A 只能是无赖：A 说谎 ⇒ "B 是无赖"为假 ⇒ B 是骑士。B 说真话 ⇒ "A 与 C 同类"为真；A 是无赖，故 C 也是无赖。检验 C：C 是无赖，其语" A 是骑士"应为假，而 A 确为无赖（假），自洽。

逐一排除其余 7 种分配（共 8 种）后，仅 (A=无赖, B=骑士, C=无赖) 一种自洽且无矛盾，故为唯一解。
