# 高难度测试卷作答（顺序 H6→H1）

## H6

【思考】
I need to evaluate six learner sentences for collocation/grammar errors.

(1) "I'm looking forward to meet you." The phrase "look forward to" is a phrasal verb in which "to" is a preposition, not an infinitive marker. Prepositions must be followed by a noun phrase or a gerund (the -ing form). Therefore "meet" (bare infinitive) is incorrect; it should be "meeting". Correct: "I'm looking forward to meeting you."

(2) "She suggested me to take the train." The verb "suggest" does not take the pattern "suggest + indirect object + to-infinitive". The grammatical patterns are: (a) suggest + gerund (suggest taking the train), or (b) suggest + that-clause with subjunctive/should (suggest that I take the train). One may say "She suggested to me that I take the train" but "suggested me to" is ungrammatical. Correct forms: "She suggested (that) I take the train" or "She suggested taking the train."

(3) "Despite of the rain, we went out." "Despite" is itself a preposition meaning "without being affected by"; it does NOT take "of". The phrase "in spite of" takes "of", but "despite" does not. Correct: "Despite the rain, we went out." (Or "In spite of the rain, we went out.")

(4) "He's capable to do it." The adjective "capable" is followed by the preposition "of" plus a gerund, not by a to-infinitive. Correct: "He's capable of doing it."

(5) "I would rather stay than to go." After "would rather" and "would rather ... than ...", the verbs are bare infinitives (no "to"). The "to" before "go" is ungrammatical. Correct: "I would rather stay than go."

(6) "It's worth to try." The adjective "worth" is followed by a gerund (or a noun), not a to-infinitive. Correct: "It's worth trying." (Also "worth it to try" is a different construction, but "worth to try" alone is wrong.)

Conclusion: all six sentences are erroneous.

【答案】
六句均不正确，错误类型与改正如下：
(1) 错误类型：介词 to 后误用动词原形。look forward to 中 to 是介词，须接动名词。正确：I'm looking forward to meeting you.
(2) 错误类型：suggest 不能接"人 + to 不定式"。正确：She suggested (that) I take the train. 或 She suggested taking the train.
(3) 错误类型：多用了 of。despite 本身是介词，不加 of（in spite of 才加 of）。正确：Despite the rain, we went out.
(4) 错误类型：capable 须接 of + 动名词。正确：He's capable of doing it.
(5) 错误类型：would rather ... than ... 后用动词原形，不应加 to。正确：I would rather stay than go.
(6) 错误类型：worth 须接动名词。正确：It's worth trying.

## H5

【思考】
The sentence is "他谁都不认识." I analyze its possible readings, the role of "谁", the source of ambiguity, and compare with "他谁都认识."

(a) Readings:
Reading 1: 他 is the subject (agent/experiencer) of 认识, and 谁 is the object. The structure is 他 + [谁都不认识], where "谁都不..." is a universal-negative construction meaning "not ... anyone". So the meaning is "He doesn't know anyone / He knows nobody." Here 谁 is a 任指 (free/universal indefinite) pronoun meaning "anyone / everybody" in the scope of negation; it is not interrogative and not a vague (虚指) use.

Reading 2: 他 is the object (patient) of 认识, and 谁 is the subject. This is a topic-fronting structure: 他(=topic/object) + 谁都不认识(=谁都不认识他). The meaning is "Nobody knows him." Again 谁 is 任指, here the universal subject "anyone / everybody" bound by 都 under negation: "Anyone does not know him" = "Nobody knows him."

So there are two readings, and in both 谁 is 任指 (universal indefinite), not 疑问 (interrogative) and not 虚指 (vague/non-specific).

(b) Root of ambiguity: Chinese has no case morphology to mark subject vs object, and relies on word order plus a flexible topic–comment structure. The bare NP 他 can be parsed either as the pre-verbal subject/agent or as a fronted topic that is the object of 认识. Meanwhile the universal phrase 谁都… can serve as either subject or object. The negation 不 with the universal 都 creates a scopal/grammatical-relation ambiguity: we cannot tell from surface form whether 他 is agent or patient. This is a syntactic/structural ambiguity (grammatical-relation ambiguity) enabled by the absence of case marking and by topic fronting.

(c) Comparison with "他谁都认识": This positive counterpart also has exactly two readings:
- Reading A: 他 subject → "He knows everyone."
- Reading B: 他 object → "Everyone knows him."
Thus the number of readings is the same (two). The difference lies in the relative salience/naturalness of the two readings, not in the count. For the negative "他谁都不认识", the agent reading (He knows nobody) is by far the default and most natural, while the patient reading (Nobody knows him) requires special context or contrastive stress. For the positive "他谁都认识", the two readings are more balanced in acceptability. So the degree of ambiguity (number of readings) is the same, but the asymmetry of preference differs. (One could also say: structurally identical ambiguity; the difference is only a matter of pragmatic preference/frequency, not of how many interpretations exist.)

【答案】
(a) 两种理解：
- 理解一：他为主语（认识的主体），谁为宾语（任指，意为"任何人"）。意思：他谁都不认识 = 他不认识任何人 / 他一个人也不认识。"谁"是任指用法。
- 理解二：他为宾语（被认识的对象），谁为主语，即"谁都不认识他"的话题前置。意思：没有人认识他。"谁"同样是任指（在"都"与否定统制下意为"任何人"）。
两种理解中"谁"都是任指，不是疑问，也不是虚指。

(b) 歧义的句法/形态根源：汉语缺乏格标记（无主宾格形态），且话题—评述结构灵活。光杆名词"他"既可分析为动词前的施事主语，也可分析为前置的话题（即"认识"的受事）；而"谁都……"这一周遍短语既可作主语也可作宾语。否定词"不"与周遍量词"都"共同造成"他"是施事还是受事的结构/语法关系歧义，表面形式无法判定。

(c) 与"他谁都认识"相比，歧义"条数"相同（都恰好两种：他主语→他认识所有人；他宾语→所有人认识他）。差别不在数量，而在两种理解的自然度/优势度不同：否定句"他谁都不认识"中"他为主语（他不认识任何人）"是默认、最自然的读法，而"没有人认识他"需要特定语境或对比重音；肯定句"他谁都认识"的两种读法接受度更均衡。因此两者歧义程度（理解数）相同，只是偏好不对称。

## H4

【思考】
We have three people A, B, C. Each is either a Knight (always tells truth) or a Knave (always lies). Statements:
- A: "B is a knave."
- B: "A and C are of the same type."
- C: "A is a knight."

I must find the unique consistent assignment and exclude the others.

Consider the two possibilities for A.

Case 1: A is a Knight. Then A's statement is true, so B is a Knave. Since B is a Knave, B's statement "A and C are of the same type" is false; therefore A and C are NOT of the same type. Since A is a Knight, C must be a Knave. Now evaluate C: C is a Knave, so C's statement "A is a knight" must be false. But A IS a Knight (by assumption of this case), so "A is a knight" is true. A Knave cannot utter a true statement. Contradiction. Therefore Case 1 is impossible; A cannot be a Knight.

Case 2: A is a Knave. Then A's statement "B is a knave" is false, so B is NOT a knave; hence B is a Knight. Since B is a Knight, B's statement "A and C are of the same type" is true. A is a Knave, so C must also be a Knave (same type). Now check C: C is a Knave, so C's statement "A is a knight" must be false. A is a Knave, so "A is a knight" is indeed false. This is consistent.

Thus the only consistent assignment is: A = Knave, B = Knight, C = Knave.

To confirm uniqueness: the case analysis over A's two exclusive possibilities exhausts all options; Case 1 is ruled out by contradiction, and Case 2 uniquely determines B (Knight) and C (Knave). No other assignment survives.

【答案】
A 是无赖，B 是骑士，C 是无赖。

推理与排除：
- 假设 A 是骑士：则 A 说真话，"B 是无赖"为真→B 是无赖。B 是无赖则其话为假，"A 和 C 同类"为假→A、C 不同类；A 是骑士故 C 是无赖。但 C 若为无赖，其话"A 是骑士"必须为假，而 A 确为骑士（真），矛盾。故 A 不可能是骑士。
- 假设 A 是无赖：则 A 说假话，"B 是无赖"为假→B 是骑士。B 是骑士则其话为真，"A 和 C 同类"为真；A 是无赖故 C 也是无赖。检验 C：C 是无赖，其话"A 是骑士"须为假，而 A 确为无赖（假），自洽。
因此唯一解：A=无赖，B=骑士，C=无赖。两种关于 A 的可能已穷尽，另一种被矛盾排除，故唯一。

## H3

【思考】
Compare (A) "He didn't reply." and (B) "He didn't bother to reply."

(a) Literal propositional content: Both sentences assert the same proposition — that he did not reply. There is no difference in the truth-conditional/literal content. The difference lies in the speaker's stance/attitude. Sentence (A) is neutral and merely reports the absence of a reply. Sentence (B), by adding "bother to", expresses an evaluative stance: the speaker implies that replying was easy or at least expected, and that the person chose not to expend even minimal effort; (B) conveys criticism, annoyance, or the judgment that the non-reply reflects negligence or inconsiderateness.

(b) Presupposition of "bother to V": This construction presupposes that the action V is trivial, easy, or low-cost — something one could do without real trouble. So "He didn't bother to reply" presupposes that replying was an easy/minor action. Implicature (conversational): It implicates that the person failed to make even a minimal effort, and thereby suggests negligence, dismissiveness, or deliberate disregard. The implicature is cancellable in special contexts but in the default case it conveys criticism.

(c) Why it cannot describe something the speaker thinks "should not have been expected in the first place": "bother to" carries the presupposition that the action was easy/expected and that not doing it constitutes a failure to make minimal effort. If the action was never expected (e.g., a baby paying a bill, or a busy celebrity personally answering every fan letter), then saying "he didn't bother to" is infelicitous because it wrongly presupposes an expectation/obligation of a trivial effort. Example where it fails: "The baby didn't bother to pay the bill." — a baby paying a bill is not something anyone would expect, so one cannot say the baby "didn't bother"; the right neutral report is "The baby didn't pay the bill." By contrast, "He didn't bother to say thank you" is fine because saying thank you is a minimal, expected courtesy.

【答案】
(a) 字面命题内容：两句相同，都断言"他没有回复"。差别在说话人立场：(A) 中性陈述、仅报告"未回复"这一事实；(B) 加入 bother to 后带有评价立场，隐含"回复本是轻而易举/理应做的，而对方连这点最小努力都不愿付出"，传达批评、不满或认为对方怠慢、不放在心上。

(b) 预设（presuppose）：bother to V 预设动作 V 是微不足道、轻松、不费力的（本可轻易做到）。因此 "He didn't bother to reply" 预设"回复是件很容易的小事"。隐含（implicate）：对方连最小的努力都不肯付出，暗含其疏忽、轻慢或故意怠慢的批评意味。

(c) 不能用于"本就不该期待发生"之事，是因为 bother to 预设该动作轻松且理应被期待，未做即构成"不肯花最小力气"的失职。若某事根本不在期待之中，则"没 bother"不成立。例：*The baby didn't bother to pay the bill.（婴儿本就不会被期待去付账单，不能说它"懒得"付）；应说 The baby didn't pay the bill. 反之 He didn't bother to say thank you 自然，因道谢是轻易且应有的礼貌。

## H2

【思考】
We form 7-digit numbers using digits 1,2,3,4,5,6,7 exactly once each. Count those divisible by 11.

Divisibility by 11 rule: A number is divisible by 11 iff the alternating sum of its digits is a multiple of 11. For a 7-digit number d1 d2 d3 d4 d5 d6 d7 (d1 leftmost), the relevant sum is (d1 + d3 + d5 + d7) − (d2 + d4 + d6) ≡ 0 (mod 11). Define S_odd = sum of digits in positions 1,3,5,7 (four positions) and S_even = sum in positions 2,4,6 (three positions). Note positions are counted from the left; equivalently from the right gives the same four-position set for a 7-digit number.

Total sum of all digits = 1+2+3+4+5+6+7 = 28. So S_odd + S_even = 28.

Bounds: S_odd is the sum of 4 distinct digits from 1..7, so minimum 1+2+3+4 = 10, maximum 4+5+6+7 = 22. S_even is sum of 3 distinct digits, minimum 1+2+3 = 6, maximum 5+6+7 = 18. Hence S_odd − S_even ranges from 10 − 18 = −8 to 22 − 6 = 16.

The condition S_odd − S_even ≡ 0 (mod 11) within [−8, 16] allows only the multiples 0 and 11 (since −11 < −8 and 22 > 16).
- If S_odd − S_even = 11: together with S_odd + S_even = 28 gives 2·S_odd = 39 → S_odd = 19.5, impossible (sums are integers).
- If S_odd − S_even = 0: then S_odd = S_even = 14. Both integers, feasible.

So the necessary and sufficient condition is S_even = 14 (equivalently S_odd = 14): the three digits placed in the even positions must sum to 14; the remaining four (odd positions) then automatically sum to 14.

Now find all 3-element subsets of {1,2,3,4,5,6,7} summing to 14:
- With largest element 7: need two others summing to 7 → {1,6}, {2,5}, {3,4} → {1,6,7}, {2,5,7}, {3,4,7}.
- With largest element 6: need two others <6 summing to 8 → {3,5} → {3,5,6}. ({2,6} invalid duplicate; {1,7} exceeds 6.)
- With largest ≤5: max sum 3+4+5 = 12 < 14, none.
Thus exactly 4 subsets: {1,6,7}, {2,5,7}, {3,4,7}, {3,5,6}.

For each such choice of the 3 even-position digits:
- Arrange them in the 3 even positions (2,4,6): 3! = 6 ways.
- Arrange the remaining 4 digits in the 4 odd positions (1,3,5,7): 4! = 24 ways.
No leading-zero issue since all digits are 1–7.

Per partition count = 6 × 24 = 144. Total = 4 × 144 = 576.

【答案】
共有 576 个。

推导：被 11 整除的判别法为奇数位数字和减偶数位数字和是 11 的倍数。设 S_奇 = 位置1,3,5,7 四数字之和，S_偶 = 位置2,4,6 三数字之和。总数字和=1+2+3+4+5+6+7=28，故 S_奇+S_偶=28。S_奇∈[10,22]，S_偶∈[6,18]，所以 S_奇−S_偶∈[−8,16]，其中 11 的倍数只有 0 与 11。若差为11，则 2S_奇=39（非整数，不可能）；故只能差为0，即 S_奇=S_偶=14。于是只需偶数位三个数字之和为14。

从{1..7}取3个不同数字和为14的子集恰有4个：{1,6,7}、{2,5,7}、{3,4,7}、{3,5,6}。每个子集对应：偶数位3个数排列 3!=6 种，奇数位剩余4个数排列 4!=24 种，共 6×24=144 种；总计 4×144=576 种。

## H1

【思考】
The sentence is from Mencius: "王顾左右而言他。" (The king looked left and right and spoke of other things.)

(a) Meaning of "他": In this context "他" means "别的（事/话）" — "other matters / something else." The king, unable to answer Mencius's pointed questions about a ruler's responsibilities, glanced left and right and changed the subject to other topics. So "他" here is not a personal pronoun but the adjective/pronoun meaning "other / different."

(b) Why it cannot be the modern third-person pronoun "he/him/that person": In pre-Qin Chinese, "他" was not used as a third-person personal pronoun. The third-person functions were expressed by 之, 其, 彼, etc. The word "他" (a variant of 佗/它) originally meant "别的、其他的" (other, another). The use of "他" as a personal pronoun (he/she/it) developed only much later, in the medieval/vernacular period, well after the Warring States era of Mencius. Therefore reading "他" as the modern personal pronoun is anachronistic and historically incorrect.

(c) Consequence of the mistaken reading: If "他" were taken as the third-person pronoun, "王顾左右而言他" would be misread as "The king looked left and right and spoke about him / that person" — implying the king turned to talk about some third party. This misses the actual point, which is topic evasion: the king dodged the difficult question by talking about other, unrelated matters ("言他" = "speak of other things"). The famous pragmatic force — the king's embarrassment and deflection — is lost if one imports the modern pronoun meaning. Thus the error is not merely lexical but distorts the intended meaning of evasion/deflection.

【答案】
(a) "他"的意思是"别的（事情/话题）"，即"其他的事"。全句意为：大王环顾左右，说起了别的事（转移话题、避而不答）。

(b) 不能理解为现代第三人称代词"他"，因为在先秦汉语中"他"并不作人称代词。当时第三人称用"之、其、彼"等表示；"他"（本字或作"佗/它"）的本义是"别的、其他的"。作为第三人称代词的"他"是后世（约中古及以后）才发展出来的用法，战国时期的《孟子》时代尚无此义，故属时代错置。

(c) 若按第三人称代词理解，整句会被误读为"大王环顾左右，说起他（那个人）"——仿佛大王转而谈论某个第三人。但实际含义是大王用"说别的事"来回避难题（"言他"=说别的事），表达的是支吾搪塞、转移话题。误读会丢失原句"避而不答、顾左右以规避"的核心意涵，把"话题转移"错解为"指称某人"。
